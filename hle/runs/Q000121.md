## RIDM 23 Application

A probability-reasoning vignette asking which single false premise
produced an agent's inflated risk estimate. Four of the five options
were close paraphrases of one diagnosis (a linear or additive growth
assumption); the fifth was semantically distinct and grammatically
rough. The cluster structure was noticed and named before ranking,
and the distinct option's keying reading was even partially
constructed mid-analysis. The commitment nevertheless went to the
cluster member that best echoed the question's criterion phrase and
carried a scope qualifier matching the stem's flagged extrapolation
move, after the distinct option was demoted by an answer-class
argument (a premise was asked for; the option read as an act).

## Framework Observations

- The reveal shows the paraphrase cluster was harvested from model
  answers, and the distinct option was the author's own verdict. The
  new option-provenance discipline had the instrument for this: the
  distinct option shares the stem's non-native idiolect, while the
  cluster is polished model prose. The typing step was available and
  was not run before ranking.
- Answer-class typing was misused as a demotion weapon against the
  author-voiced option. On a stem written in rough idiolect, a class
  mismatch inside an option is predicted noise from the same pen, not
  typed refutation.
- Paraphrase multiplicity is itself provenance evidence: an idea
  restated across four options with micro-variations is the filtered
  population's modal answer, and a unique-key menu cannot sit inside
  an undifferentiated cluster. Intra-cluster differencing (scope
  qualifiers, criterion echoes) is rationalization fuel until the
  out-of-cluster option's keying reading is constructed and refuted
  at parity.

## Lessons

Candidate lesson: on menus where most options restate one idea and
one option stands apart, the cluster marks the harvested modal
answer; type provenance by idiolect first, and treat intra-cluster
scope differencing as unlicensed until the distinct option is
constructed to parity and refuted.
