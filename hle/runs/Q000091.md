## RIDM 20 Application

A six-panel figure of simulated spin dynamics: three observables per panel, one
panel showing a physically valid evolution. The curves were extracted
programmatically (axis calibration from tick marks, color-mask extraction,
legend regions masked), which produced clean time series and honest per-panel
diagnostics. Two panels were eliminated on hard range violations. The
remaining elimination rested on an exact-consistency invariant that holds for
a two-level system: the entropy is a deterministic monotone function of the
polarization vector's length. One panel tracked that function oscillation by
oscillation in one entropy base; the rest did not, so that panel was committed.

The keyed panel was a different one. The item was generated from an actual
simulation of a three-level system with several deliberately modified
dynamics, and the key follows the generator's unmodified case. Under three
levels the committed invariant does not apply at all: the entropy is not a
function of two expectation values, the elimination dissolves, and the
apparent oscillation-by-oscillation match was a numerical coincidence on a
wrong model.

## Framework Observations

- The decisive evidence was on the table before commitment and was explained
  away. The three initial values were exhaustively proven impossible for any
  two-level state under every labeling convention. That proof refuted the
  two-level frame itself, and the spec's own menu-mismatch rule generalizes to
  exactly this case: a given that is impossible under the working model
  reopens model identification, not the plot's credibility. Instead the
  impossibility was discharged as a shared plotting artifact because it
  appeared in all six panels, and the refuted frame was retained.
- The initial values were an exact algebraic fingerprint of the true system:
  simple half-integer and root-two-over-two values that are the matrix
  elements of the next spin representation up. Exact rational or algebraic
  givens that a working model cannot reproduce are model-identification
  evidence, and the model variant space (system dimension, composite
  structure) was never enumerated even though the frame-variant discipline is
  standing.
- A discovered numerical near-coincidence (one panel's entropy matching the
  two-level formula in one base across nine samples) was promoted from
  hypothesis generator to discriminator, against the standing rule that
  coincidences discriminate nothing. It felt like designed-detail payoff; a
  payoff read inside a frame that cannot explain the item's own givens
  inherits the frame's refutation.
- Rigor-cap compliance failure, independent of the frame error: the item was
  explicitly classified as generated during analysis, and the generated-class
  band cap (medium) was then not applied at commitment. The banding checklist
  ran, but the cap check was reasoned around because the instrumented
  elimination felt derivation-grade. Caps are ceilings on the referent, not
  on the derivation, and instrument quality cannot buy them back.
- What the generated-item discipline would have predicted: one panel is the
  generator's unmodified textbook simulation and the others are narrative
  modifications; the unmodified case is the panel that looks most like the
  canonical damped-oscillation figure. That chain points at the keyed panel
  without any of the machinery that misfired.

## Lessons

Candidate for the sprint file: an impossible given refutes the frame, not the
figure. When stated or plotted givens are provably unrepresentable under the
working model across all conventions, the model's scope is refuted; enumerate
model variants that render the givens exactly, treating exact algebraic
fingerprints as identification evidence. An alarm of this strength may not be
discharged by attributing sloppiness to the artifact while the frame it
refutes is retained. Separately: apply the generated-class band cap
mechanically once the classification is made.
