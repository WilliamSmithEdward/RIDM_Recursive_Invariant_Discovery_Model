## Framework Observations

- Among conflicting recalled table cells, the ranking instrument
  should have been source authority of the anchor: an international
  standard's published cell is the modal referent for a taught-course
  item, above a reconstructed web-resource table, even when the
  terminology points at the latter's author. The two sources shared
  an author here, which dissolved the identification argument the
  commitment rested on.
- The round-answer collapse was used as a route selector among recall
  variants. The coincidence rule already forbids this for readings;
  the same discipline applies one level down, at selection among
  conflicting recalled values.
- The low band was correct and priced the loss: conflicting
  unverified recall variants on an exact-match cell is a low-band
  configuration by rule, and the audit holds.

## Lessons

Candidate lesson: when conflicting recalled values for a published
table cell survive, rank them by the citability and authority of the
source each is anchored to, standard over reconstruction, and treat
a round-number collapse under one variant as hypothesis fuel, never
as the selector.
