## Framework Observations

- This is a third distinct oracle regime across the runs: a well-formed but under-determined
  key. The task's stated success criterion (an exact count) exceeds what the task statement
  determines: the true answer is a random variable plus unstated conventions. No reasoning

- The specification's clarification threshold (Section 9) covers ambiguity of meaning, but

  setting it caps attainable confidence structurally.
- Confidence behavior was correct for the regime: the band was stated, the risk named, and

## Lessons

1. Name the criterion-information mismatch regime: when the required precision exceeds what
   the task statement determines (stochastic outcomes, unstated seeds, versions, or
   conventions), recognize it explicitly, report the central estimate with its band, flag
   the mismatch, and cap confidence at what the answer distribution's concentration
   supports. Convention uncertainty usually dominates sampling noise.
