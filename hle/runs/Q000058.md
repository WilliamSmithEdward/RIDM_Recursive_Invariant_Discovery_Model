## Lessons

1. In game positions, run a board-wide threat census before evaluating any move:
   enumerate every existing three-in-a-row for both sides with its completion square
   and that square's gravity dependency, then evaluate moves against the census;
   stacked threats exist only at census level.
2. Solve-for-all phrasing over moves means all optimal first moves, the domain's
   standard reading, not the moves of one chosen line.
