## RIDM 30 Application

A geometric series whose ratio is a quadratic in a quantity built from a
"three-digit number" whose leading digit ranges over a signed interval
that excludes zero. The item was typed casually authored: interval
notation over what the stem calls digits, a decoy leading coefficient,
and a leading digit allowed to be negative so that convergence is
possible at all. The convergence condition reduced to two short open
intervals for the built quantity, and the reading forks were enumerated
before ranking: digits versus continuous uniform draws, and, for a
negative leading digit, the place-value formula applied with the signed
digit versus the sign applied to the whole three-digit string. All
three readings were computed to exact answers, the two digit readings
by exhaustive enumeration and the continuous one in closed form.

The commitment took the place-value formula with the signed digit,
argued as the canonical expansion of a three-digit number and the
formula an author would code. The key holds the sign-applied reading:
its listed convergent cases are exactly the nine enumerated under that
reading, all of them negative numbers with three digits.

## Framework Observations

- Candidate coverage held: the keyed value was computed to exact form
  before commitment and demoted at the ranking step.
- The demotion was a fired-verdict override. The coherence rule had
  reached a verdict: under the committed reading most of the built
  numbers are not three-digit numbers at all, so the stem's own type
  declaration was false for most triples, while under the rival every
  built number is a genuine three-digit number. That verdict was noted
  and then overridden by an author-process prior about how the formula
  would be coded, which is a meta-prior the standing rules say breaks
  ties and never overturns.
- A stated type for a constructed object is a given like any other. A
  reading under which the construction fails to be an instance of the
  declared type fails the representability audit, and the "canonical
  formula" argument was a mechanism-level canon transplanted from the
  nonnegative-digit setting where it coincides with the type claim.
- The rigor cap and the medium band were right; the ranking inside the
  band was wrong.

## Lessons

- A type the stem declares for a constructed object is a given under
  the representability audit: a reading that makes most instances fail
  the declared type is refuted by that failure, and a formula that is
  canonical in the setting where it coincides with the type claim does
  not carry that canonicity into a setting where it does not.
- An author-process prior ("they would have coded it this way") is a
  meta-prior, not typed evidence, and cannot override a fired coherence
  verdict.
