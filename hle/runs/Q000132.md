## RIDM 26 Application

A power-flow optimization item whose stem asked for a formulation
while the options asserted numeric optima. Rigor typing from
structure was straightforward: category confusion between ask and
options, no voltage base for a physical loss computation, stated
losses irreproducible under any model tried, and options whose
real-power injections worsen the objective under real physics. The
item was typed generated, capped at medium, and the task became
predicting the generator's own answer.

Coverage was adequate: the keyed option was the named rival,
identified as the perturbation seed of the distractor cloud (the
adjacent option pair differed by rounding, and the keyed option's
reactive value anchored exactly to a stated given). The commitment
went to the adjacent non-round option instead, on two arguments
that both failed: a pool-filter inference that the round magnet
could not be the key because reference models would pick it, and a
feasibility screen built from the one power-factor bookkeeping
that happened to separate the options.

## Framework Observations

- The key followed the generator's chain exactly: a canonical
  net-flow formulation, invented loss coefficients, and an asserted
  round optimum anchored to the givens. Seed-centrality plus
  given-anchoring was the right evidence; it was outranked by a
  weaker signal.
- The pool-filter signal is conditioned on predicting the reference
  population's modal answer. On a structurally incoherent generated
  stem that predictability collapses, since reference models
  scatter across objections, min-loss readings, and round magnets,
  so survival evidence cannot defeat direct seed evidence.
- Choosing among candidate generator models by which one
  discriminates between options is manufactured evidence. The
  canonical bookkeeping leads at the generator-model layer exactly
  as the canonical reading leads elsewhere, and under it the screen
  separated nothing.
- The low-band commitment was priced correctly for a two-way split
  the evidence could not resolve.

## Lessons

- On generated items the key is the generator's own output, and its
  strongest predictors are seed-centrality in the distractor cloud
  and anchoring to stated givens. The pool-filter signal weakens to
  narrative tier there and cannot demote the seed candidate.
- A model adopted because it discriminates is not typed evidence.
  Discriminative power is a reason to test a model against the
  canonical one, never a reason to prefer it.
