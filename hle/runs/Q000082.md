## RIDM 19 Application

A photograph of an ornate baroque apparatus with five candidate identities.
The commitment route was a census of visible mechanisms rather than a gestalt
match: a capstan of turned handles on a geared housing, a long metal barrel,
and a sphere on a legged stand with a valve fitting. Each menu option was
evaluated clause by clause for its mandatory features: the heat-engine
option lacks any boiler or firebox, the electrostatic option lacks a glass
friction element and wheel drive, the optical option's tube terminates in a
gear housing rather than optics, and the planetary-model option lacks arms
and gearing. One reading consumed every visible element, with the barrel as
a piston cylinder, the capstan as its rack drive, and the sphere as a
receiver on its plate, and that reading was committed.

## Framework Observations

- The absent-mandatory-feature audit did the discriminating work: the two
  tempting distractors (the optical tube, the sphere) each borrow one
  element of the apparatus, and both fail on features their identity cannot
  do without. Probing by technique fingerprint rather than surface
  association, the discipline from earlier attribution losses, carried a
  trivia-category item that the category's record made a prior hazard.
- Whole-menu coherence earned the high band honestly: one reading explained
  every element with no leftovers, and each rival was refuted by observable
  absence rather than by a plausibility judgment.

## Lessons

No new framework lesson. The run confirms the mandatory-feature audit and
fingerprint-over-association disciplines on image identification items.
