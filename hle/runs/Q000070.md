## RIDM 17 Application

A manufacturing puzzle: maximize value cutting spheres, or pairs of half-spheres
at lower value, from a hollow square profile whose wall thickness exactly equals
the sphere diameter and whose every dimension is a multiple of it. The committed
reading: the material tiles perfectly into sphere-sized cells, the half-sphere
option is strictly dominated cell-wise, so the answer is the tiling count at
full value. The key instead stagger-packs layers at reduced spacing, accepting
fewer whole spheres in the offset layers and cutting half-spheres in the corner
conflict zones the offset creates, which nets more total value than the perfect
tiling.

The decisive failure was rival strawmanning, the exact catalogued mode: the
staggered packing was found during analysis, evaluated as whole-spheres-only,
scored below the tiling, and pruned. The half-sphere recovery that makes it win
was never attached to it, so the ranking compared the author's construction
minus its key mechanism against the leading candidate at full strength.

## Lessons

1. A capability the stem grants (an operation, a product form, a move type) is
   construction: a committed solution under which it is never used is an
   undischarged alarm, and the rival that uses it must be computed to parity
   before ranking.
2. Dominance pruning must compare whole solutions, not components: an option
   dominated per-unit can still be globally optimal when it extracts value from
   regions the dominant option cannot reach.
3. An answer option lying inside the solver's own derived feasibility window,
   above the committed value, is a flagged observable: reproduce the
   construction that would achieve it or refute it before committing below it.
