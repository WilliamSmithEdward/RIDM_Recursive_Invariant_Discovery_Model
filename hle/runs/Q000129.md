## Framework Observations

- The adjacent-canon motif did not corroborate the recall, it
  corrupted it: when a recalled specific attribute coincides with a
  more famous neighboring work's motif, that coincidence is
  contamination evidence about the recall itself, because the famous
  motif is the availability magnet acting at the memory layer. It
  was allowed to add comfort instead of subtracting confidence.
- Coverage failed one level down from usual: the candidate set held
  a single recalled variant, and the keyed near-species rival was
  never generated. A per-clue probe of the symbol's semantics (a
  stinging, burning, proletarian weed rather than a merely prickly
  one) plausibly generates the rival; the probe was never run
  because the recall arrived feeling like a title.
- Recalled titles carry their exact noun the way recalled constants
  carry their parameterization; a one-noun title recall without an
  independent separating probe is low-band material, and the medium
  commitment overpriced it.

## Lessons

Candidate lesson: when a recalled attribute is corroborated by a
more famous adjacent work's motif, type the corroboration as
contamination, enumerate the attribute's semantic near-neighbors as
rivals, and commit at low unless an independent probe separates
them.
