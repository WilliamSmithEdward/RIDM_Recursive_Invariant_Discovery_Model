## RIDM 29 Application

The product of a diene with a tetrahalogenated ethene. The
availability magnet is the [4+2] adduct; the recalled result, a
classic of physical organic chemistry, is that this particular
alkene adds to dienes by a stepwise diradical route and gives the
[2+2] vinylcyclobutane almost exclusively. The recall was checked
against structure rather than trusted on familiarity: the
regiochemistry follows from which halogenated carbon carries the
odd electron in the intermediate, the dichloro end being the better
radical site, so the difluoro carbon bonds to the diene terminus and
the dichloro carbon closes onto the vinyl-bearing carbon. The
commitment named the compound with lowest-locant numbering and
stated the bonding explicitly, so the claim was one molecule.

The key names the same molecule with a non-lowest locant for the
vinyl group; the bonding pattern is identical.
