## RIDM Application

The vignette decoded into a long causal chain of diagnosis, drug, side effect, counter-drug,
complication, and management change. Every link in the chain up to the final one was
identified correctly, confirmed by the official rationale after reveal.

The failure was at the last inference. A mid-scenario complication inverted the sign of the
patient's underlying problem: the state the counter-drug had been suppressing became, after
the complication, the state that needed restoring. The attempt carried the stale sign
forward and read the final management change as replacing the withdrawn drug's action, when
the coherent reading was the opposite: supporting what the withdrawn drug had suppressed.

Worse, the attempt noticed the contradiction explicitly (the chosen reading made no
physiological sense after the complication) and resolved it by assuming the scenario author
was being loose, rather than treating the incoherence as evidence against the chosen reading.
A second, high-availability domain association reinforced the wrong reading because it
pattern-matched a famous fact and echoed a theme planted earlier in the scenario.

## Framework Observations

- Section 39 requires recomputation after material observations, but it is framed for
  actions. In pure reasoning, a stated mid-scenario state change is the same trigger: every
  downstream claim that depends on the changed state must be recomputed, including signs and
  directions. The stale-sign bug is the reasoning-side twin of the static-materiality
  failure mode.
- Interpretation selection needs a coherence gate. When a leading interpretation renders the
  stimulus internally incoherent, that incoherence is evidence against the interpretation.
  Noticing a contradiction and overriding it by downgrading the source's coherence is a
  recognizable failure signature.
- Availability is not evidence. A famous association that pattern-matches the surface of the
  problem, plus a thematic echo from earlier in the text, produced a narrative-tier inference
  that outranked the coherence check. Narrative-tier support should be the weakest claim
  grade, and it must not survive contact with a contradiction.

## Lessons

1. Coherence gate on interpretations: prefer the reading that makes the scenario internally
   coherent; treat self-noticed contradictions as refuting evidence, not as author noise.
2. Propagate state changes through the claim graph in pure reasoning, exactly as Section 39
   demands after actions; recompute signs and directions, not just facts.
3. Grade availability-driven associations and thematic echoes as the weakest support tier;
   they may suggest hypotheses but never resolve conflicts.
