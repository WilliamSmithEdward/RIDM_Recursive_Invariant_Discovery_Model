## RIDM 19 Application

A field-theory exercise: Gaussian-integrate the phonon fields out of a
deformation-type electron-phonon coupling and report the induced
electron-electron interaction for a single wavevector. The coherent-state
Gaussian integral was carried through from scratch, with the polarization
sum collapsing the coupling squared to a clean quadratic form and the two
members of each momentum pair combining the single-pole propagators into
the standard symmetric denominator. The result was cross-checked by a
second route, the textbook matrix-element-squared-times-phonon-propagator
form, and its static limit against the canonical-transformation result.

The known hazard was the factor-of-two convention: the pair interaction
counted once versus the summand of a full symmetric momentum sum. Both
forms were committed with their exact scopes named, per the
representation rule. The key holds the full-sum summand form, exactly the
named variant, in the Matsubara representation also committed. The key's
answer field drops the coupling's square, but its own rationale carries
the square throughout, as the Gaussian integral forces; the committed form
carries it.
