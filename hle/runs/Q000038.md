## RIDM 14 Application

A data-interpretation item: three soundscapes, two treatment conditions, six daily
percentages each, and eight options mixing habitat claims with disturbance claims. The
attempt derived each option's truth value from the data directly: control attraction
identified the natural habitat and its settlement window, the treatment's early reversal
established the disturbance claim, and a near-chance series established a non-habitat.
Every specific option contained at least one clause contradicted by the data. That reduced
the menu to the generic true statement versus none-of-the-above, and the attempt committed
to the generic truth on a design argument: an author keying none-of-the-above does not
include a demonstrably true statement in the same menu, and under the stated criterion a
true statement defeats the none option. The reveal confirmed both the data reading and the
adjudication, in the same order.

## Framework Observations

- The none-of-the-above adjudication has a structural rule: the none option is refuted by
  any option whose truth is decidable from the given data, however vague. Vagueness is not
  falsity; under a choose-the-correct-answer criterion, scope-humble truths dominate.
- The earlier lessons composed well here: the absent-feature audit (no control attraction
  to one soundscape) killed the habitat claims that included it; per-option truth
  evaluation against a single consistent reading of the data handled the rest; the
  settlement-window pattern (early attraction fading with age) was read as signal rather
  than noise.
- The item's presentation was sloppy but its data were decisive, and the rigor-matched
  ranking from the previous run applied cleanly: no text repair was needed, just literal
  clause-by-clause evaluation.

## Lessons

1. Evaluate none-of-the-above last, and only after every other option's truth value is
   settled from the given evidence; a single surviving true option, however generic,
   refutes it.
2. In multi-clause options, a single false clause kills the option; evaluate clauses
   independently against the data before any holistic plausibility judgment.
