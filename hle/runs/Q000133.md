## RIDM 26 Application

A multi-step structure chain: hydrocarbon plus bromine giving one
product, then excess ammonia, elemental analysis pinning a
saturated diamine, a stated spectral signal count, then nitrous
acid, then hot acidic oxidation yielding an acid plus carbon
dioxide, with a neutralization mass fixing the acid. The elemental
analysis and the acid identification (with a rounded-volume
discharge of a small mass mismatch) were both correct and matched
the key's own reasoning. The commitment failed at two linked
layers upstream.

First, the signal-count convention. Counting every hydrogen
environment, the plain reading, leaves exactly two candidate
skeletons, neither of them mine. I resolved the fork by dropping
the exchangeable protons, the one convention under which my
leading candidate survived, and justified it post hoc. Second, the
keyed skeleton was in my candidate set and was demoted on an
untyped feasibility assertion about the first step, imported from
research-grade selectivity data, against an item whose curricular
canon treats small-ring opening as clean alkene-like addition.

The stem also carried a mechanism hint I left decorative: an acid
formed with gas released upon heating is the thermal
decarboxylation signature of a 1,3-diacid, which back-propagates
through the whole chain to a 1,3-difunctional skeleton. My reading
survived deletion of the heating qualifier; the key's consumed it.

## Framework Observations

- Same failure shape as the previous run, on a different layer:
  a bookkeeping choice (there a power-flow model, here a spectral

- Canon indexing was half-applied: the item's provenance was

## Lessons

- Resolve counting and bookkeeping conventions canonically before
  ranking, and enumerate candidates under every live convention
  symmetrically. A convention chosen because it rescues the leading
  candidate is motivated bookkeeping, the ranking-side twin of the
  manufactured discriminator.
- A qualifier paired with an observable (heating with gas release)
  is a mechanism hint under the deletion test; commit only readings
  that give it causal force.
- Feasibility and selectivity claims against a school-canon item
  are typed by the curriculum, not the research literature; a
  clean single-product step the curriculum teaches cannot be
  demoted by real-world mixture data.
