## RIDM 27 Application

A formula-plug item: given the level count, size ratio, entry count,
and write-buffer size of a log-structured merge tree, find the
minimum entry size. The item's vocabulary matched a well-known
systems-paper cost model, so the level-count formula was taken from
that family. Three variants of the formula were enumerated and their
thresholds computed: the paper's version with its (T-1)/T factor,
the bare version without it, and the exact per-level capacity sum.
The boundary convention (does the threshold value itself count as
the minimum, or the next integer) was resolved toward the threshold
on the ground that the givens were engineered to make it a clean
integer.

The key uses a fourth variant, a buffer-inclusive cumulative
capacity that puts an additive one inside the logarithm, with the
strict boundary, so its answer is one above a threshold my variant
set never produced.

## Framework Observations

- The convention space of a formula family was enumerated
  incompletely: the additive-constant variant (cumulative capacity
  counted with the buffer) is a standard member of that family and
  was not generated. Coverage failed at the convention layer, the
  layer the frame-as-candidate rule says to enumerate exhaustively.
- The integrality argument was a null discriminator. Because the
  entry count and buffer size were both powers of two, every
  variant's threshold was an integer, so the "designed clean number"
  selected nothing among variants and nothing about the boundary,
  yet it was used to pick the inclusive boundary over the canonical
  strict reading of a ceiling formula. A coincidence that holds under
  every candidate is not a designed detail.
- The canonical boundary reading of a ceiling-of-logarithm level
  count is strict, and the key applied it. The override was a
  selection by elegance, the sprint-0014 blind-selection failure in
  a new costume.
- The low band was correct pricing for a fork the evidence could not
  resolve; the loss is a coverage loss, not a calibration loss.

## Lessons

- A formula-plug item is keyed by the author's variant of the
  formula; enumerate the family's published variants, including
  additive-constant forms, before ranking, and treat a threshold
  that is an integer under every variant as discriminating nothing.
- A boundary convention is resolved canonically from the operator
  the formula uses (a ceiling makes the threshold exclusive), never
  from the roundness of the threshold.
