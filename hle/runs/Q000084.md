## RIDM 19 Application

A minimum-seed problem for threshold-3 cellular infection on a square grid
of stated even size. The rigorous route ran end to end and was entirely
self-contained. Lower bound: an edge-demand count (every infection consumes
three infected-neighbor edges) gives three times the seed count at least
the site count plus twice the side length plus the number of seed-seed
adjacencies; a boundary analysis (corner sites can never be infected,
healthy runs of length two on a side can never fill, and an exactly
alternating side forces a seed-corner contact) forces at least four
seed-seed adjacencies on even sides, lifting the bound by exactly one seat.
Upper bound: small cases were brute-forced exactly (four sizes) and
annealed to the bound (three more), which exposed the optimal structure, a
parity checkerboard with every third row emptied except one alternating
end seed plus two corner repairs. Scaling that structure to the stated
size produced an explicit witness that a simulator verified percolates,
with seed-seed adjacencies exactly at the forced minimum, saturating the
bound. The key's rationale uses the same perimeter argument, an equivalent
formula, and essentially the same banded construction.

## Framework Observations

- Bound-plus-witness carried the run: neither half alone would have earned
  the high band, since a plausible unrefined bound sat one seat lower and a
  naive one-step construction sat far higher. Both defective-key candidates
  were computed and priced before the reveal (CL49), which cost nothing and
  would have made any defect differencing immediate.
- Small-case exhaustion before formula-guessing was decisive: the first
  guessed formula died at the first even case, the second at the annealed
  sixth case, and the structure that survived came from reading the
  annealer's witnesses, not from a remembered result. Instrumented search
  as hypothesis generator, exact proof as validator.
- An annealer stall one seat above the bound was correctly treated as
  absence of evidence: the structured witness was built from the smaller
  exact solutions rather than trusting the stall as a floor.
