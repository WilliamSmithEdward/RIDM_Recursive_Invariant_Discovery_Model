## RIDM 16 Application

A nine-slot attribution task over an image grid of works by modern masters, graded on
the full ordered list. Slots were generated independently per the compound-answer rule,
and the joint band was honestly priced near the product of slot confidences, which the
outcome validated. Five slots matched. The four misses decompose into two failure
classes. First, ensemble meta-priors overrode per-slot evidence: one slot's immediate
style read was the correct author, demoted solely because another slot already used
that author, and the key indeed repeated the author; two further slots had the keyed
authors in their candidate sets, demoted by a set-coherence prior about what kind of
collection the grid was. Second, subject-association probing displaced style probing:
two slots were probed by asking who paints that subject rather than whose hand that is,
while the stem explicitly flagged style as the criterion, and the keyed authors are
canonical precisely for the technique fingerprints visible (line quality, ground tint,
palette), one of them absent from the candidate set entirely.

## Framework Observations

- The no-repeat assumption is the compound-answer twin of sophistication capture: an
  unstated ensemble constraint (distinct authors, coherent era, curated diversity) is a
  meta-prior, and it cannot override a slot's own canonical style read. The existing
  diversity-audit rule covers ensembles assembled for variety; this run shows the dual
  direction, imposing variety the task never promised.
- Attribution items key on hand, not subject. Subject-to-painter association is
  availability anchoring in this domain, because famous subjects attract many painters
  while technique fingerprints are near-unique. The stem's own criterion hint named
  style, and the deletion test would have caught the under-consumption: several
  commitments would have been identical had the hint been absent.
- Calibration behaved: the very-low band priced the compound risk correctly, and the
  slot-level hit rate landed inside the stated expectation. Honest joint pricing on
  compound recall is now twice-validated.

## Lessons

1. On compound answers, per-slot evidence outranks ensemble priors: never demote a
   slot's leading read because of what another slot already used, and treat unstated
   distinctness or coherence constraints as narrative-tier.
2. On attribution-by-style tasks, probe by technique fingerprint, not subject
   association; ask whose hand made this before asking who paints these things.
