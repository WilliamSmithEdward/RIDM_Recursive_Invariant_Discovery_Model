## Framework Observations

- In benchmark settings the operative success criterion is agreement with the key, so the
  decisive claim behind every commitment is a prediction of the key, not the attempt's own
  theorem. Internal validation (bound plus witness, convergent routes) exercises the
  conditional branch; it says nothing about the branch condition when the author's model is
  unrecovered.
- Informal puzzle wording, casual framing, and round budgets are provenance signals
  (Section 19) that the key follows an intended construction rather than an adversarially
  tight statement. On such items, reconstructing the author's likely construction is a
  required coverage probe, and availability is legitimate evidence about the author even
  though it is not evidence about the ground truth.
- A noticed-but-unresolved coherence alarm (the constraint that never binds) was allowed to
  dissolve instead of capping confidence. Alarms that cannot be discharged must be priced.

## Lessons

1. On informally authored maximization puzzles, derive the answer and independently
   reconstruct the author's most available intended construction; when they diverge, the
   divergence is the dominant residual and the band drops to medium or below.
2. A stated constraint that stays slack under every reading the attempt can construct is
   evidence the author's reading is missing, not evidence the constraint is decorative.
3. Bound-plus-witness certainty applies to the attempt's formalization only; the map from
   formalization to key is a separate claim that needs its own support.
