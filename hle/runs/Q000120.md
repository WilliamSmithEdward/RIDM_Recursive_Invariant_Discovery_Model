## RIDM 22 Application

A path-diagram sign-prediction item over a nectar-alkaloid pollination
system probed by a knockout manipulation. The five path signs were
generated slot by slot from identified sources rather than from one
narrative: a published memory-enhancement result anchored the retention
path and its downstream benefit, a published handling-time result
anchored the negative sign on per-flower visit duration, the
time-budget trade-off set the duration-to-yield sign, and the direct
path was typed as the classic production-cost residual. The assembled
vector was then checked as a whole story: both behavioral channels
positive in composition, direct cost negative, which is the canonical
plants-drug-their-pollinators narrative. The all-positive option was
identified as the naive attraction magnet, options inverting the
retention pair were refuted by the memory literature, and the
impossible-to-tell option was refuted by the stem's own most-likely
framing.

## Framework Observations

- Slot-by-slot generation with per-slot anchors, then whole-vector
  coherence as a check rather than a generator, is the right order for
  compound sign answers: the reveal's rationale matched every slot's
  stated mechanism.
- The knockout framing was consumed as scope evidence (normal-to-none
  contrast licenses using natural-concentration literature), which the
  key's rationale states explicitly in the same terms.

## Lessons

No new failure lesson. Win pattern: on path-sign vectors, anchor each
edge independently in its own literature before assembling, and let the
assembled story's coherence confirm rather than choose.
