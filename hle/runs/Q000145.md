## RIDM 27 Application

A provenance recall item: which museum acquired a named interwar
New Objectivity painting in a stated year. Recall entered the ledger
typed as reported and was checked against structure rather than
vividness: the painter worked and died in the eastern German state,
the stated acquisition year falls in that state's period, and the
capital's national gallery was the institution buying that movement
in those years. Near neighbors were generated as rivals (the
painter's home-town collection, the two other large eastern
galleries) and ranked below the recalled anchor on that structural
fit. The commitment named the institution with its parent body,
which is one entity with the key's East-Berlin qualifier.

## Framework Observations

- The recall-variant discipline worked as written: anchor the
  recalled attribution to independently supported structure (place,
  period, collecting pattern) instead of upgrading it on
  familiarity, and generate the adjacent collections as rivals
  rather than leaving them unlisted.
- Pricing at the low end of the medium band was right for a single
  recalled attribution; the hit does not license a higher band on
  recall-only items.

## Lessons

No new failure lesson. Win pattern: on provenance recall, rank the
recalled institution by fit to place, period, and collecting
behavior, and name the adjacent institutions as explicit rivals.
