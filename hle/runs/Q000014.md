follows the correct value; per Section 48 the signal is internally reconstructible and the

## Framework Observations

- A new shade of L20 in the same sprint: the failure was not copying a foreign constant but
  rounding away a self-derived one to match a remembered round number. Precision hygiene is
  part of the derivation-over-recall discipline: an intermediate constant snapped to a
  rounder recalled value silently swaps a derived claim for a reported one mid-chain.
- The criterion-information mismatch handling otherwise worked: the risk was named in
  advance, confidence was capped low, and the miss occurred exactly at the named risk.

## Lessons

1. Carry derived precision to the end of the chain: never round an intermediate constant to
   agree with a remembered round value; the remembered value may serve as an anchor check
