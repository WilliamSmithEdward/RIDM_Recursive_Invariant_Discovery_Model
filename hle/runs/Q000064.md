## RIDM 17 Application

A counseling vignette whose surface offers several plausible medication
interactions but whose construction encodes one diagnosis: a sensory percept with
no possible physical source, experienced away from its associated setting and
followed by a headache, with a planted misattribution the patient herself makes.
The given-role partition resolved it cleanly: the narrative givens are diagnosis
layer (they establish an aura-preceded migraine and exist only for that), and the
decision layer is one item on the medication list whose drug class is canonically
contraindicated by that diagnosis. The committed counsel was to stop that product
and switch to its progestin-only alternative, which the key matched verbatim in
substance.

Rival counsel candidates (an analgesic-antidepressant bleeding interaction, a
caffeine note) were computed and demoted because they consume none of the
elaborate sensory construction; under the frame discipline, a reading that leaves
the whole constructed scenario decorative loses to the one it was built for.
