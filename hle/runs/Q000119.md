## Lessons

Candidate for the sprint file: on telescoping or stepwise
transformations (sequential cyclizations, condensations,
substitutions), the candidate space is the extent-of-reaction ladder,
one candidate per rung, with rungs matched to the stated conditions'
harshness; the famous endpoint is the availability magnet, mild stated
conditions are an extent-limiter flag, and a surfaced recall that the
endpoint's canonical prep is harsher than the stem's conditions is
typed refutation of the endpoint, not a doubt to average away.
