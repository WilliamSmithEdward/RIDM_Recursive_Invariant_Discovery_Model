## RIDM 19 Application

A minimum-cost sensor-coverage item: a rectangular area, three sensor types
with different footprints and prices, a fractional coverage threshold, and
sensor centers quantized to a coarse grid. Two frames were constructed: an
additive-coverage integer knapsack in which footprint areas simply sum, and
a geometric frame in which disjoint placement inside the walls binds. The
rigorous geometric route was actually run: the maximum number of large
sensors that fit disjointly inside the walls on the grid was derived, the
hexagonal arrangement was constructed explicitly, the clean rectangular fill
slots were counted, and a near-complete fill design was costed. The attempt
then demoted that completed route on an author model, classifying the item
casually authored from its prose register (grammar slips, flavor framing)
and predicting the author solved the additive knapsack. The knapsack
optimum, attainable three ways, was committed at medium band.

The key held the geometric construction: the same large-sensor packing the
attempt had derived, the same count of square fill units, plus a handful of
small circles in pockets, at a total cost a few hundred below the attempt's
own second-ranked geometric design. The key's rationale states outright
that models answer with the additive count and that this is the planted
error.

## Framework Observations

- Costume rigor classification, the exact failure CL30 names: rigor was read
  off grammar and register while the checkable structure said purposeful
  construction. The coverage threshold binds tightly under the geometric
  frame (margin under half a percent in the key and in the attempt's own
  fill design) and is slack under the additive frame; a stated requirement
  that constrains the answer only under one frame is a frame discriminator,
  and it was in hand before commitment.
- Derivation-precedes-author-modeling was violated in a new direction: the
  rigorous route did not fail, it succeeded and was then overridden by a
  modeled author naivety. CL29 licenses the author-error model only on
  failure of the rigorous route; a successful rigorous construction is the
  referent basis, not a rival to be out-psychologized.
- The grid-quantization constraint was consumed by the geometric frame and
  decorative under the committed one; the committed answer survives deletion
  of the grid note, failing the deletion test. Same for the anti-model
  population prior: the additive optimum's triple-tie cleanness was read as
  robustness when, in a pool filtered so strong solvers fail, cleanness
  under the naive frame is magnet evidence.
- The geometric work itself was sound: sensor-count maximum, arrangement,
  and fill-slot census all match the key's construction; the key's small
  refinement (small circles in pockets the squares cannot enter) was noted
  during analysis and discarded with the frame.

## Lessons

1. When the rigorous route completes, it is the referent basis; an author
   model may cap its band but may never displace it in favor of a naive
   shortcut the author is merely presumed to have taken. Prose costume
   cannot license the displacement.
2. A stated threshold that binds tightly under one frame and hangs slack
   under the other is a frame discriminator, checkable before commitment.
