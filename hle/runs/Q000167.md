## RIDM 30 Application

A five-year search-interest chart with three series and the question of which
country hosted the three historic events the series track. The chart was
instrumented rather than read by eye: each series was extracted per pixel column by
color, the axis was calibrated from the three date labels and cross-checked against
the vertical data-collection note the charting service draws on a known date, and
peaks were located programmatically. Each series peaked in the same calendar week
every year, and the three anniversary weeks fell in one country's calendar of
remembrance days. Amplitude confirmed it: two series jumped in round-number
anniversary years of the events they track. The cross-series spikes closed the case:
one series jumped in the week a former head of state associated with one event died,
one series jumped when a film about the coup preceding that event was released, and
two series jumped in the same week in the autumn of the final year, matching a
literature Nobel awarded to an author whose two best-known novels are about those two
events.

The commitment named the country with the three events identified. The key names the
country and the same three events.

## Framework Observations

- Instrumentation converted an eyeballed reading into observed dates at weekly
  resolution, and the calibration cross-check caught a small label offset that would
  otherwise have left the anniversary weeks one week off.
- Coverage ran per clue: the annual peak weeks alone admitted a second country whose
  national remembrance day falls in the same week as one of the three, and the other
  two series and the cross-series spikes discharged that rival.
- The key's rationale says identifying the events is unnecessary and the anniversary
  pattern suffices; the attempt identified them anyway, which is what made the rival
  country's discharge typed rather than presumed.

## Lessons

- None new. The run repeats the instrument-first pattern for chart items, with the
  addition that a chart's decorative-looking annotations, a provider's note line, are
  calibration anchors.
