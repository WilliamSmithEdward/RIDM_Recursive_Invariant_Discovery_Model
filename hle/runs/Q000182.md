## RIDM 32 Application

A casually authored puzzle: an operative names a meeting point with a single Cyrillic
word, the engineer objects that it is the word for a medical state, and the operative
answers that the place is present on the map though unlabelled and that the engineer
already has everything needed. The code points of the word were inspected as a flagged
observable and found to be genuine Cyrillic. Coverage then generated two families: the
pun family, in which the word is the English word for a punctuation mark and the mark's
character code or shape selects a region; and the encoding family, in which the letters
are homoglyphs of Latin letters whose character codes are coordinates. The encoding
family was computed in two variants, all uppercase and all lowercase, both of which gave
values that did not read as a latitude-longitude pair, and the family was set aside. The
pun family was committed: the mark's code selects one parallel that crosses exactly one
option, and that option is also the small unlabelled enclave with a mark-shaped outline.

The key is the region selected by the encoding family in the variant that keeps the
stem's own casing: a capital first letter and three lowercase letters, whose codes split
into a latitude in the far north and a longitude in the same district.

## Framework Observations

- The stem's casing is a given and was perturbed under enumeration. The encoding family
  was run only in the two uniform-case variants; the case-faithful variant, the one the
  stem actually presents, was never computed, and it is the one that yields a pair of
  in-range coordinates. A family discharged on variants that alter a given has not been
  probed.
- The pun was scene-setting and was bound as the mechanism. The deletion test would have
  said so: remove the exchange about the medical state and the puzzle still works from the
  letters, the engineer, and the map; remove the engineer's profession and the encoding
  route dies. The profession was the mechanism-naming hint, and the joke the operative
  laughs at was the decoy.
- The plain programmer route was displaced by a more elaborate story. The homoglyph
  reading was the first thing inspected and was dropped as "not obviously helpful"
  before its case-faithful variant existed; the committed route needed a pun, a character
  code, a parallel, and a shape argument, and its designedness was taken as support.
- Candidate coverage held. The keyed region was named as the target of the lowercase
  variant; the loss was at variant enumeration and at the mechanism-hint binding.

## Lessons

- Enumerate an encoding family at the stem's own case, spelling, and character
  inventory before any normalized variant; a family is discharged only after the
  given-faithful variant is computed.
- On a casual stem, the mechanism-naming hint is the detail whose deletion kills the route;
  a joke the stem itself deflates is scene-setting, and building the commitment on it is
  decorative consumption.
