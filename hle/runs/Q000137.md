## RIDM 26 Application

An eleven-clue composition puzzle whose final answer required
synthesizing sub-answers through a wordplay equation and a second
possessive chain. Eight of the eleven sub-clues resolved correctly
against the key, several exactly (the mistranslated club nickname,
the middle-alphabet etymology, the slogan substitution, the
foreword anecdote, the free-models story). The composite layer,
a multilingual name-forging chain through a metallurgical pun into
a ballet character and a poet's pseudonym, was never reached, and
the committed terminal word came from the wrong branch of an
ambiguous middle link.

The diagnostic detail: on two sub-clues the keyed answer was
generated first, from the strongest plain evidence, and then
displaced by a later, cleverer-feeling construction. The subject's
actual combined naval-military title was derived and abandoned for
a more designed-feeling numismatic etymology; the plain reading of
a declaration against a pseudonymous impostor (a namesake denial)
was generated and dropped for a thematically wittier aphorism.

## Framework Observations

- Third instance this sprint of one failure shape: a selection
  layer resolved by appeal to effect or cleverness rather than
  canonical precedence. Here it was recall-variant selection, the
  layer Control Law 20 explicitly covers, and the law lost to a
  designed-feeling coincidence exactly as written not to.
- The low band was priced correctly, and the bounded-budget stop
  was right: the composite layer sat beyond closed-book reach
  regardless of process. The recoverable loss was only in the
  displaced sub-answers.
- Candidate coverage at the terminal layer: the keyed word never
  entered the set. Coverage of the sub-clue layers was strong,
  which is what makes the displacement pattern, not generation,
  the operative failure.

## Lessons

- Protect first-generation candidates: a candidate produced from
  the strongest plain evidence is displaced only by typed evidence
  at matching scope, never by a later rival whose sole advantage
  is feeling more designed, more thematic, or more clever. This is
  the recall-layer form of the sprint's recurring motivated-
  selection failure.
