## RIDM 26 Application

A minimum-resource guarantee question over soft-label prototypes
and a distance-weighted classifier, with the answer expected as a
formula over the stem's symbols. The source family was identified
correctly, and substantial instrumented probing (search and
asymptotics inside a self-built formalization) mapped the
feasibility landscape of my model of the classifier. The committed
answer came from the identified source's headline theorem, at the
low band because the theorem-scope identification stayed weak.

The keyed answer was a formula over a different stem symbol
entirely, reached by an anchor-localization analogy: the label
vector treated as distance signals, unique localization in D
dimensions requiring D plus one anchors. The keyed formula
appeared in the first candidate brainstorm and was dropped
unprobed. Every candidate that survived to ranking consumed only
the class-count symbol; the dimension and dataset-size symbols did
no work under any of them, and their deletion would have changed
nothing about the committed answer.

## Framework Observations

- Criterion-referent blindness, by its catalog name: the attempt
  validated its own formalization at length while the authored key
  reasoned in another frame. Instrumented evidence inside a
  self-chosen formalization cannot adjudicate an authored-referent
  question when the formalization is itself the uncertain layer,
  and deeper instrumentation anchors the wrong frame harder.
- The stem's symbol inventory was the unread map: an answer-formula
  item declares its candidate families through the parameters it
  bothers to introduce. A prominently typed parameter that no live
  candidate consumes is a missing candidate family, exactly as an
  unused given is a missing reading.
- The low-band commitment priced the uncertainty honestly; the
  loss here is a coverage loss, not a calibration loss.

## Lessons

- Extend the unused-givens audit to the symbol level for
  formula-answer items: enumerate which stem symbols each candidate
  consumes, and treat a dangling introduced symbol as a generator
  for a new candidate family before ranking.
- A candidate generated in brainstorm and never probed is a silent
  coverage failure; the gate's consumption check should count
  dropped first-pass candidates.
- On casually authored guarantee questions, reconstruct the
  author's intended chain from the stem's emphasized machinery
  before spending instrument budget on one's own formalization.
