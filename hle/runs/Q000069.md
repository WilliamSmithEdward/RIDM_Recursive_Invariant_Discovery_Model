## RIDM 17 Application

A compute-the-dimension item resting on recent research-level results: the
bounded cohomology of a product of two copies of a circle-acting group in a fixed
degree. The commitment decomposed the claim into two halves of different claim
types. The lower bound was derived: given the single-factor polynomial-ring
theorem, the three pullback cup-product classes were shown independent by
restricting to the two factors and the diagonal, a self-contained argument. The
upper bound (no classes beyond the Euler-class algebra) was recalled from the
recent product computations in the bounded-acyclicity literature, supported by
the mechanism (stabilizers of the product action are products of boundedly
acyclic groups, and bounded acyclicity is closed under finite products), and the
key cited exactly that literature. The dimension count then reduced to counting
degree-four monomials in two degree-two generators.

## Framework Observations

- Splitting a single numeric answer into a derived half and a recalled half let
  the band be set honestly at the weakest link (the recalled upper bound) while
  the derivation guarded against the most likely error modes (under-counting by
  treating the product factor-wise, or over-counting phantom classes).
- Mechanism-level support for recall earned its keep: remembering why the
  product computation works (bounded acyclicity of stabilizers, closure under
  products) distinguishes a reconstructible theorem from a bare fact, and that
  distinction is what separated this commitment from unverified single-fact
  recall committed low by rule.
- A cross-check noted that the naive Kunneth count agrees with the theorem here,
  so even the wrong route lands on the right value; the run recorded the
  agreement but kept the commitment on the theorem-backed route, since the
  agreement is a coincidence of this degree and group, not support.

## Lessons

1. When an answer rests on a recalled research-level theorem, split it into the
   largest derivable fragment plus the minimal recalled remainder before
   banding: a derived lower bound or partial structure both raises the floor of
   the answer's reliability and localizes exactly which recalled claim the band
   is betting on.
