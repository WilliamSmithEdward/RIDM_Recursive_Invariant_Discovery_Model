## RIDM 18 Application

A raw insect gene sequence with a menu pairing protein functions to source
organisms. The observation was instrumented rather than eyeballed: the open
reading frame was translated programmatically, verified stop-free at the
expected length, and scanned for family signatures. Four independent
canonical motifs of one enzyme family were observed at their expected
positions, from the N-terminal signature through both catalytic-residue
motifs to the C-terminal motif, upgrading the function claim from inferred
to observed.

The menu was then evaluated clause by clause against observed properties:
one option refuted by amino-acid composition (the repetitive-protein option
requires several-fold higher glycine and alanine content than measured),
one by length (the yolk-protein option implies a protein four times
longer), one by absent mandatory features (the structural-protein option's
chitin-binding consensus is missing while catalytic motifs are present),
and one by plain function mismatch. Exactly one option named the observed
enzyme family, so the organism claim rode the forced function match, with
the genus fitting the item's framing and the domain's literature. The key
confirmed both function and organism, citing the source accession.

## Framework Observations

- Instrumentation converted the decisive claim's type. Translating and
  motif-scanning in code produced observed evidence where mental
  translation would have produced inferred evidence, and the high band
  rested on that upgrade plus motif independence.
- Composition and length checks were cheap quantitative refuters for two
  options that qualitative reasoning would have dismissed less decisively;
  measuring beats characterizing even when the direction seems obvious.
- The compound answer (function plus organism) carried asymmetric support,
  strong derivation for function, menu-forcing for organism, and the
  single-hydrolase menu structure meant the weaker component could not
  change the selection. Componentry risk was priced and found immaterial.

## Lessons

No new framework lesson. Instrumented observation, absent-mandatory-feature
audits, and clause-by-clause menu evaluation, all existing disciplines,
carried the run.
