## Lessons

Candidate for the sprint file: on multi-slot commitments, the band and the
stopping rule key on the weakest slot's margin-to-noise ratio, not on the
strength of the global model that pinned the easy slots. A measured value
landing between adjacent hypotheses at the instrument's noise scale is an
in-window ambiguity: escalate to a stronger instrument or cap the band;
nearest-neighbor plus a one-to-one constraint is a ranking, not a
measurement.
