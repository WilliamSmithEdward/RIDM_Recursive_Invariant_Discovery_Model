## Lessons

1. Conventional-association precedence: on authored interpretation items, a symbol's
   keyed significance is its standard cultural association, selected by the nearest
   complete local context; a property-mapped or aesthetically superior reading is
   inference-tier and may not outrank the convention.
2. This sprint's canonical-route precedence generalizes across domains: curricular
   chain in medicine, textbook definition in genetics, course procedure in engineering,
   received symbolism in poetry. The ranking layer must ask first what the standard
   association is, and only break ties with sophistication.
