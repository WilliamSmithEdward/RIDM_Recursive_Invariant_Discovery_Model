## RIDM 17 Application

A dialogue-costumed geometry item: an image of packed circles and rectangles on a
stated half-unit grid, most absolute measurements withheld, with three usable givens
(one small shape's diameter, a congruence link between two shape classes, and a
Monte Carlo hit count over the whole area). The intended chain was to invert the
Monte Carlo fraction into the total area, snap the enclosing dimensions to the grid,
and let the packing geometry force every remaining coordinate. The grid constraint
did real work: the only admissible row spacing made the packing fill the height
exactly, which pinned the target center without any tangency assumption.

Instrumentation replaced eyeballing per the observation contract: a local
pixel-classification script measured every component's centroid and bounding box,
and all shapes snapped to the grid within 0.02 units, with the scale cross-checked
against both the border and the given small-shape diameter. The reveal matched the
committed pair, and the key's rationale followed the same area-inversion chain.

## Framework Observations

- Instrumented observation carried the run. Freehand pixel reading during the first
  pass produced a spurious edge gap that briefly suggested a non-tangent, off-grid
  layout; the script's measurements dissolved it. The upgrade from inferred to
  observed was material, not cosmetic.
- Convergent routes closed cleanly: the given-data chain (hit count to area to
  forced packing) and the direct measurement chain agreed on every coordinate.
- The unstated coordinate-origin convention was the weakest decisive link. It
  discharged by symmetry: the target center sat on the vertical midline of the
  area, making the answer invariant to the plausible origin choices. The alarm
  ledger closed with all entries discharged.
- The reported hit count equalled the exact expectation of the implied fraction,
  a tell that the author computed the expectation rather than sampling; this
  confirmed the area chain as the intended construction.
- Given-consumption audit passed: every dialogue given (diameter, congruence link,
  refusal to give more, hit count) was consumed causally by the committed chain.

## Lessons

1. When a stated discretization constraint (a grid, a unit step) interacts with a
   packing or layout, test whether the constraint alone forces the configuration
   before assuming tangency or symmetry from the picture; a forced configuration
   upgrades the claim from inferred to derived and can neutralize residuals, such
   as an unstated origin, that would otherwise cap the band.
