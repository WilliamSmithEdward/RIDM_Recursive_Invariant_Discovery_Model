## RIDM 18 Application

A puzzle agent may compute only with fractions whose integers stay within a
stated bound, and must estimate a physical mass within a stated error budget;
the graded answer is the largest integer in the minimal such calculation. The
derivation layer went well: the exact-data route with the coarsest constant
approximation produces an output fraction that violates the workspace bound
(a subtle representability trap), the legal outputs inside the error window
were enumerated exhaustively, and both surviving route families were
constructed to parity with all intermediates checked legal, including the
route the key turned out to hold.

The commitment failed at frame selection. Two frames were live: represent
the given data exactly and spend the error budget only on the incomputable
constant, versus treat the givens as approximable inputs under the overall
budget and minimize the integer bound globally. The second frame admits an
error-cancellation route with a much smaller largest integer, and it was
committed on the argument that the cancellation was the puzzle's designed
aha, with the stem's repeated minimization phrasing as supporting evidence.
The key holds the first frame: data exact, only the constant approximated,
its representation forced by output legality, largest integer set by the
given density's only faithful representation.

## Framework Observations

- This is sophistication capture operating one level up, at the frame rather
  than the reading. The design-elegance argument (the cancellation is too
  neat not to be intended) is exactly the meta-prior class the canonical-
  precedence law demotes to tie-breaking, and it was allowed to overturn the
  plainer frame instead.
- The keyed construction was in the candidate set, ranked second, demoted by
  that meta-prior: the sprint-0005 signature recurring at the frame layer.
- The canonical content missed: in a calculation over given data,
  "estimate" licenses approximating what cannot be represented, the
  irrational constant, and does not license corrupting the given quantities,
  which have exact faithful representations in the workspace. The
  minimization clause then operates inside that frame, not over it.
- The low band was calibration-honest for once: the referent spread was
  recognized as wide, the modal choice was stated with its probability, and
  the loss lands in the band whose audited record expects it.

## Lessons

1. On calculation items over given data, the canonical frame represents the
   givens exactly at the item's own precision and spends the error budget on
   constants that cannot be represented; a route that perturbs givens to
   optimize a secondary objective is a rival requiring explicit license from
   the stem, and never the lead on a design-elegance argument.
2. A discovered cancellation, coincidence, or forced-move chain that makes a
   sophisticated route feel designed is a hypothesis generator about the
   author, not a discriminator between frames: the same evidence is equally
   consistent with an author who never left the plain frame.
