## RIDM 22 Application

A count-the-correct-statements item over an author-presented theorem
about coarsening and refining constants in a logic program, with four
skeptical critiques and one affirmative interpretation on the menu. The
rigorous route ran first: the theorem's mathematics was verified
directly (the expansion operation is order-independent under
per-occurrence tracking, the coarsen-after-refine composition is exactly
the identity, and the commutation of inference with granularity checks
out on positive programs by lifting and projecting derivations). That
settled three critiques as unfounded. The decisive judgment was the
fourth critique, which is literally true mathematics about the dual
composition the claim never asserts: the referent question was whether
the author would credit its literal truth or read it as the refuted
misconception. Style-corpus evidence resolved it: the affirmative
option shares the stem's distinctive idiolect, marking it author-voiced,
while the critiques read as harvested model outputs the author planted
to dismiss; and the affirmative option's own wording directly
contradicts the true-but-tangential critique, so an author who wrote one
would not key the other.

## Framework Observations

- The reveal confirmed the full author model: the rationale dismisses
  all four critiques as AI misunderstandings, including the
  literally-true one, on exactly the grounds predicted (its concern
  "does not apply" under the stability condition). Author-voice
  fingerprinting via shared idiolect between stem and option was the
  discriminating instrument, an application of the stem-as-style-corpus
  discipline to option provenance rather than construction slots.
- Verifying the theorem before modeling the author mattered: it
  converted three demotions from plausibility judgments to typed
  refutations, leaving only one genuinely contestable statement, and the
  count answer then hinged on a single referent prediction instead of
  five.
- The literalist rival (crediting the tangential critique's truth, count
  two) was constructed and priced, not just dismissed: its keying
  reading required an author who plants a deliberately-true critique
  among harvested doubts, which the item's dismissive framing and the
  option idiolect both disfavored.

## Lessons

No new failure lesson. Win pattern: on statement-evaluation menus where
critiques were plausibly harvested from models, type each option's
provenance by idiolect against the stem; an option in the author's own
voice is the author's verdict, and a literally-true critique of a
proposition the claim never asserts is keyed by the author's reading of
it, not by its truth value.
