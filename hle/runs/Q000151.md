## RIDM 29 Application

A screenshot from planetarium software showing a wide-field night
sky with a handful of labelled planets and bright stars, a landscape
silhouette at the bottom edge, and a timestamp in a named European
summer time zone. The question asked for the country of the
viewpoint. The item was typed well-posed: the sky at a stated
instant fixes the observer's latitude and longitude, so the task is
a measurement over an artifact and the framework's instrumentation
rule applied. The observation was instrumented rather than
eyeballed: bright blobs were extracted from the image, six labelled
or unmistakable objects seeded a least-squares fit of observer
latitude, local sidereal time, image rotation, projection centre,
and scale, with the projection family and the sky-view mirroring
enumerated as candidates rather than assumed. The mirrored
stereographic model won by a factor of thirty in residual, and the
fit was then widened to sixty-eight catalogue stars, matched
automatically from the model's own predictions, closing at a tenth
of a pixel. Sidereal time against the timestamp's UTC equivalent
gave the longitude; the latitude came straight from the fit. The
point sits well inside one country's interior, several degrees from
any border, so the country was committed at the high band.

## Framework Observations

- Enumerating the projection family and the mirror state as
  candidates, rather than defaulting to the first fisheye guess,
  was the difference between a nonsense fit and a sub-pixel one.
  The first fit, with one convention assumed, produced an
  unphysical latitude; the representability failure refuted the
  model, not the data.
- The planets' approximate ephemerides were excluded from the final
  fit and used only as a sanity check; their residuals of a dozen
  pixels were explained by the recalled ephemerides, an alarm
  discharged by explanation.
- The key's stated locality lies about two hours of sidereal time
  east of the fitted longitude, in the same latitude band. The
  offset is consistent with a time-zone bookkeeping difference on
  the author's side and is immaterial at the asked grain, which the
  key's own rationale says was chosen for exactly that reason.
