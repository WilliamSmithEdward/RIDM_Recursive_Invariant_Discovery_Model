## RIDM 20 Application

An eight-slot matching task: eight non-circular roller pairs against eight
cumulative angular displacement curves. Rather than eyeball lobe counts, the
entire system was reproduced: both rollers' radius profiles were extracted
from the figure by ray casting from detected rotation-center markers, the
rolling contact was simulated as an ODE in two variants (a conjugacy model
using center distance, and a two-profile arc-length model), and the
assignment was solved exhaustively over all permutations for both rotation
senses and several window lengths.

Two extraction defects surfaced and were fixed before commitment because
intermediate results were checked rather than trusted: the figure's panel
rows are unequal heights, so assumed-uniform cropping corrupted half the
curves (caught by a bimodal cost pattern that made four fits perfect and
four garbage); and the circled panel labels leaked into the curve color
mask, injecting spikes (caught by overlaying matched wobbles). After the
fixes, the same assignment won under every model variant, sense, and the
correct window, with per-slot runner-up margins of 4x to 100x, and the key
matched exactly.

## Framework Observations

- The previous run's lesson was applied directly: the weakest slot's
  margin-to-noise ratio was computed before banding (4x at worst, against
  the prior run's 1.5x), and the earlier in-window ambiguity pattern was
  absent, so the high band was earned rather than assumed.
- The decisive matching signal was the detrended wobble waveform, not the
  raw curve: cumulative integration compresses a ninefold speed variation
  into a few-percent ripple, so raw-curve distance was noise. Choosing the
  representation in which the discriminating physics lives is part of
  instrument design.
- Anomaly-driven debugging carried the run: a cost matrix that fits half
  the items perfectly and half at chance is an extraction-defect signature,
  not a matching result; the uniform mediocre fit after the first fix was
  the second signature. Both alarms were chased to mechanism instead of
  being accepted.
- Convergent structure checks (dominant harmonic of each roller's radius
  function versus each curve's ripple count) independently corroborated the
  simulated assignment on the clear slots.

## Lessons

Reinforces, rather than adds to, two standing disciplines: instrument the
observation and validate the instrument's intermediate products against
ground truth before reading its output; and band compound answers at the
weakest slot's margin-to-noise ratio, escalating the instrument when margins
sit at noise scale.
