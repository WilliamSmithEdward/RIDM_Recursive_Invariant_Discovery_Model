## Lessons

- When a stem is written in capability language, the frame is the
  domain's capability criterion and the key follows it; an
  empirical-literature rival needs typed evidence from the stem, not
  an author-population prior, to displace it.
- Rules that reach a verdict bind at commitment. Three rules
  reaching the same verdict with none binding is a gate execution
  failure, not a coverage failure.
