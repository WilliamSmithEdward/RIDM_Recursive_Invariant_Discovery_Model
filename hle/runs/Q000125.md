menu of ten algorithm sketches. The decisive structural fact, that
covering paths may share vertices, was read off the stem's own
worked example, which selects the chain-cover reduction (transitive

## Lessons

No new failure lesson. Win pattern: on algorithm-selection menus,
run the leading option and its nearest rival on the stem's own
worked examples before ranking; a rival that fails the stem's
