## RIDM 21 Application

A nine-option menu about a famous monument, with most options built as
source sentences carrying planted errors. Clause-by-clause falsity ranking
killed seven options cleanly, including one by a chronological derivation
(a claim attributed to an ancient author who died before the described
event) that the key's own rationale confirms. The committed option paired a
genuine scholarly analysis with a famous documentary fact; the key instead
selects a comparative judgment option, sourced to a named monograph, that
the commitment had generated as a possibly-lifted quote and demoted as
subjective.

## Framework Observations

- The committed option carried a noticed alarm that was charitably
  discharged: the documentary clause's placement and completeness wording
  deviated from the ancient testimony (text displayed on bronze at the
  entrance, not inscribed on the structure), and that deviation was exactly
  the author's planted error. Under falsity-before-coverage, a noticed
  wording deviation in a menu built of planted-error options is the error
  hypothesis, not a looseness to forgive; discharging it charitably
  repeated the Control Law 38 failure in option-local form.
- The stem's criterion phrase asked for the analysis with most backing, and
  precisely one option was a pure interpretive judgment rather than a fact
  recital. The criterion wording was an unconsumed flag typing the answer
  class; the deletion test on the word "analysis" would have shown the
  committed reading ignoring it.
- The rival that keyed was generated (recognized as a likely quotation from
  a specific text) and demoted with an untyped plausibility judgment,
  repeating the run-101 pattern: Control Law 45 demands typed evidence, and
  a recognized-quotation hypothesis is support for a rival, not against it.
- What worked: the chronology kill on the nearest factual rival was
  derivation-grade and correct per the key's own rationale, and the
  planted-error menu model was the right structural reading of the item.

## Lessons

Candidate for the sprint file: on planted-error menus, a noticed wording
deviation inside the leading option is the planted error until proven
otherwise, and an option recognized as a lifted quotation gains standing
from that recognition. Type the answer class the stem's criterion phrase
asks for (fact recital versus interpretive judgment) before ranking
options across classes.
