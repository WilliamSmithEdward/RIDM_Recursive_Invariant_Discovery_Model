## RIDM 17 Application

A learnability item over a standard embedding-composition scheme: which boolean
relation operators a linear classifier over the composed features can and cannot
represent, split between same-dimension and cross-dimension variants. The
derivation enumerated the effective feature algebra per case: for same-dimension
targets the composition's absolute-difference and product features hand the
classifier the two nonlinear primitives directly, so every operator in the family
linearizes; for cross-dimension targets those pairing features decouple from the
target, leaving only the raw coordinates, where the two parity-type operators are
the classically non-separable ones and the threshold-type operators remain
separable. The committed option was the unique two-element set.

## Lessons

1. On learnability-of-operator items, write down the effective feature set per

   threshold-type operators survive.
