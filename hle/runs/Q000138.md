## Framework Observations

- The pool-filter discipline failed by scope, not by absence: the
  rule fires on split-verdict items, and this was a split-verdict
  statement inside a multi-select menu. On such menus the filter
  check runs per statement, since each statement has its own
  reference-population modal answer, and the one aligned with a
  field-wide gloss is the designed trap.
- Paired with the earlier run this sprint where the filter signal
  was over-applied on a generated item, the boundary sharpens: the
  filter's strength tracks the item's rigor class and the
  predictability of the population's answer, weak on incoherent
  generated stems, decisive on expert-authored statements that
  contradict a standard account.
- A causal explanation claim is verified by toggling the claimed
  cause in a constructed variant, never by quoting the field's
  gloss. The internal tension was even surfaced during analysis,
  licensing versus causing, and was discharged by authority appeal
  rather than by the cheap construction.

## Lessons

- Run the filter check per statement on multi-select menus; a
  statement restating the field's standard account is the
  consensus magnet in statement form.
- Verify because-claims by construction: toggle the claimed factor
  and watch the quantity. A recalled gloss about causes is
  narrative-tier against a one-step counterexample.
