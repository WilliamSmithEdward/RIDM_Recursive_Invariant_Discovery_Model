## RIDM 31 Application

A classroom-style consulting scenario: a small firm implementing knowledge management for
the first time to lift a sales result, an illustration of its sales funnel with the
documents each stage group needs, three named models each glossed in a sentence or three,
and a menu of the three singles, two pairs, and an all-of-the-above. Typed casually
authored from the register and the adapted diagram, so the key holds the author's
intended construction and the band caps at medium. Coverage ran over all six options. The
illustration's documents fit the tacit-to-explicit model, its prospect list and staged
progression fit the data-to-wisdom model, and its interview and work-manual items give the
educational taxonomy a hook, so three options stayed live: the documentation model alone,
the two organizational models together, and all three.

The pool-filter check then decided. The reference population's modal answers are the pair
and the single, since the educational taxonomy reads as a red herring to a solver who
applies domain judgment, and the item's survival is typed evidence that the key sits
outside that modal set. Two menu observations pointed the same way: the singles-pairs-all
architecture is the classic all-of-the-above pattern, and the author gave the educational
taxonomy the longest and most careful gloss, which is not how a throwaway distractor is
written. The all-of-the-above option was committed at the low band, with the pair as the
priced rival.

The key is all three, and its rationale is a strategic plan applying each model in turn,
with the taxonomy assigned to continuous learning.

## Framework Observations

- The filter signal was licensed and load-bearing. The stem is coherent and the menu
  funnels a judgment-applying solver to one of two options, so the population's
  convergence was predictable, and the item's survival was read at that strength rather
  than at narrative tier.
- The domain-rigor reading, that an educational taxonomy is not a knowledge-management
  model, was exactly the reading the population would take, which is what made it the
  wrong referent prediction. Author-fit outranked domain rigor on an authored referent,
  as the framework directs.
- Two alarms were bound rather than dismissed: the singular "what model" in the question
  and the phrase noting the taxonomy's educational origin. Both discharged under the
  committed reading as casual-register background, and the band stayed low because they
  were the rival's best evidence.
