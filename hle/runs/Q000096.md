## RIDM 20 Application

A fifteen-slot matching task between short-horizon and long-horizon
time-averaged cellular automaton patterns from the same unknown rules. The
attempt invested heavily in generative identification: extract all thirty
panels, infer the seeds, brute-force the rule space, simulate forward, and
match. Six rule-space hypotheses were tested; five panels identified exactly
and six more up to families, but four panels were proven unreproducible in
every tested space, so the generator's convention was never fully captured.

Late in the run, the decisive instrument finally surfaced: the long-horizon
average provably contains the short-horizon frames as a fixed fraction of
its weight, so direct high-passed correlation between the two groups
measures the pairing with no generative model at all. That instrument,
solved as a pure global assignment, produced the fully correct mapping on
every contested slot. The committed answer instead blended it with texture
scores from the refuted rule-space model, which flipped two slots, and a
manual single-entry override of the solved assignment flipped a third.
Twelve of fifteen slots matched; strict grading scores the tuple.

## Framework Observations

- Evidence from a refuted model is inadmissible everywhere, not only on the
  items that refuted it. The rule space was demonstrably unable to reproduce
  four panels, which is proof that identifications and simulations built in
  that space carry unquantified error; their scores still entered the final
  blend at full weight and overrode a direct measurement that was right.
  The claim-type hierarchy already covers this: the relic correlation was an
  observation of the actual relation between the two groups, while the
  simulation scores were derivations inside an unsupported model, and a
  derivation in a refuted model does not outrank an observation.
- Check for a direct measurement of the target relation before
  reverse-engineering the generator. The containment relation between the
  two horizons was derivable from the stem alone and implementable in
  minutes; hours of generative identification preceded it. The route chosen
  first was the more impressive one, not the more evidential one.
- A solved global assignment is a joint object: a manual override of one
  cell, justified by one matrix entry, silently reallocates the displaced
  labels and broke a complementary slot. Overrides of a global optimum must
  re-solve the assignment, and a single-entry argument against a
  jointly-optimal solution is narrative-tier.
- Banding was honest: the weakest slots were named at commitment, the low
  band was assigned, and the loss landed in-band. The failure is in
  aggregation discipline, not calibration.

## Lessons

Candidate for the sprint file, two entries. First: once a model is refuted
by any subset of the data, everything computed inside it drops to
narrative-tier everywhere; direct measurements of the target relation
outrank refuted-model derivations at any blend weight, and blending them is
scalar laundering of a failed evidence gate. Second: on assignment tasks,
seek an instrument that measures the pairing relation itself before
attempting generative identification of the underlying process; and never
hand-edit a jointly-optimal assignment cell by cell.
