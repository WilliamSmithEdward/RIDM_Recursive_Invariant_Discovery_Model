## RIDM 31 Application

A well-posed textbook item: a named stereoisomer of a halogenated methylcyclohexane
treated with a bulky alkoxide base, asking for the product's name. The route was
mechanical and was run twice on independent machinery. By hand: the descriptor pair
resolves to the cis diastereomer, the reactive chair puts the halide axial with an
anti-periplanar hydrogen available on both neighboring carbons, and the bulky base takes
the less hindered one, giving the less substituted alkene while leaving the
methyl-bearing stereocenter untouched with its priority order unchanged, so the
descriptor carries over. By instrument: a cheminformatics library enumerated the four
stereoisomers, embedded each to read the substituent dihedral, built both elimination
products by editing the graph, and assigned descriptors, agreeing with the hand route on
every point, including a borderline embedding that the enantiomer-pair invariant
resolved. The commitment named the product with its descriptor, with the achiral name and
the minor alkene scoped as representations.

The key is the same name; its rationale gives the same two reasons, the less hindered
site and the preserved stereocenter.
