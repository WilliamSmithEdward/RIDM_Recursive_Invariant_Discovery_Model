# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0011 (Q000101 to Q000110)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 21, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L80 through L85 are consolidated into RIDM 22, released 2026-09-02.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0012.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: six of ten runs scored, with the high band a clean four of
four, and all four misses landed one layer above the instrument disciplines RIDM 21
hardened, at referent-variant coverage. In every miss the keyed answer was generated or
cheaply generatable, and it was lost at the demotion or enumeration step: a keyed count
was demoted with an untyped "no reason," a sign-convention fork was covered with a fake
fork (two rearrangements of one expression), a degraded hand-implementation of a
published algorithm was never generated as a candidate referent, and a noticed wording
deviation in the leading option of a planted-error menu was charitably forgiven while
the rival recognized as a lifted quotation was demoted on plausibility. The wins were
carried by the standing disciplines: exhaustive search and mechanical simulation with
anchor validation, identified-source recall with menu coherence, designed-detail fork
resolution, and correctly scoped consumption tests.

## L80. Rivals fall only to constructed readings (Q000101, Q000105)

RIDM 21 surface: Sections 23 and 24, Control Law 45. A candidate count was demoted with
the untyped phrase "no reason to fix one channel," and a menu option recognized as a
likely quotation from a specific text was demoted as subjective. Both keyed. Typed
demotion has a constructive prerequisite the spec did not state: before a candidate
value, count, or option is demoted, construct the reading that would key it, and demote
only when that reading is refuted by typed evidence. Recognition that an option quotes
an identifiable source is standing evidence for the option, not against it. On counting
items specifically, the identification scope (which symmetries, labels, rotations, or
channel assignments identify two objects) is a frame variant space, and each candidate
count is keyed by one scope; ranking counts without constructing their scopes is
ranking strawmen.

## L81. Consumption tests bind only where readings differ (Q000101, Q000109)

RIDM 21 surface: Sections 23 and 24. The same deletion test was decisive in one run and
wrong in another. In the miss, both live readings consumed the tested given (a fixed
cyclic labeling), so the test had no discriminating power, and treating it as decisive
manufactured support for the committed reading. In the win, exactly one reading consumed
the tested given (a cardinality that only the nonzero-count reading uses), and the test
correctly demoted the rival. The precondition is checkable before use: a consumption or
deletion argument ranks two readings only when they differ on the tested given.

## L82. Enumerated forks must span distinct conventions (Q000102)

RIDM 21 surface: Section 30. A formula answer differed from the key by overall sign
because the stem-defined symbol admits two sign semantics (thermodynamic chemical
potential versus positive expenditure), and the key used the other one. A fork was
enumerated at commitment, but it was two algebraic rearrangements of one expression,
which covers nothing. Fork enumeration is coverage only over semantically distinct
bookkeepings, and for sign forks there is a cheap selector: determine which convention
makes the stem's stated values come out with their stated signs, and commit one claim
with both conventions named and scoped when the stem underdetermines it.

## L83. The referent is an implementation, with predictable defects (Q000103)

RIDM 21 surface: Sections 19, 21, and 23. A compute-metric-per-source item was answered
with a faithful reconstruction of the published algorithm, validated on anchors; the key
hand-implements a degraded variant in which an exclusion clause is transplanted from
sum scope into the parameter definitions. The candidate-referent set held only faithful
convention forks, never the predictable misreading. On items graded by an author's
implementation of a published procedure, the misreading classes of the procedure's
definition are candidate referents in their own right, the most transplantable clause is
the modal defect, and the band prices referent fidelity rather than derivation
strength. This generalizes trap pricing from shortcut-taking authors to
mis-implementing authors.

## L84. On planted-error menus, the noticed deviation is the plant (Q000105)

RIDM 21 surface: Sections 23 and 24, Control Laws 38 and 53. A menu was correctly
recognized as source sentences with planted errors, seven options were killed cleanly,
and the committed option's own noticed deviation (a placement and completeness wording
against the ancient testimony) was charitably discharged as looseness. It was the
planted error. Once a menu is classified planted-error, a noticed wording deviation
inside the leading option is the error hypothesis and kills or caps until refuted;
charitable discharge is the option-local form of alarm dismissal. Separately, the
stem's criterion phrase types the answer class: a question asking which analysis has
most backing points at interpretive-judgment options, and ranking fact recitals against
judgments without typing the asked class skips a flagged observable.

## L85. What worked: exhaustible spaces, anchors, identified sources (Q000104, Q000106 to Q000110)

RIDM 21 surface: Sections 16, 19, 39, and 40. Sizing the state space first turned an
optimization puzzle into a complete breadth-first derivation with an anchor-validated
solver and a legality-checked witness; a bespoke-notation simulation was verified
mechanically after consuming the stem's format examples as convention tests; a
curricular vortex model was anchor-validated on the single-body limit, its givens
verified by deletion, and its clean rational collapse read as route confirmation while
the no-ground magnet was computed and priced. On the recall side, two wins came from
identifying the item's source community or edition before ranking (a dress-hierarchy
corpus; a classic syntax text selected over an earlier-publication rival by stem
quantifiers and answer-shape fit), and one from pinning a cardinal exactly by matching
a translation injection against a total-map count. Six of six wins were instrument-led
or identified-source; all four losses were referent-variant misses.
