# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0009 (Q000081 to Q000090)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 19, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L69 through L73 are consolidated into RIDM 20, released 2026-09-01.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0010.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: eight of ten runs scored, the second-best sprint on record,
and the derivation, instrumentation, referent, and representation disciplines all carried
weight. The two misses share one shape: at the commitment step, completed or standing
structure was displaced by an unlicensed meta-frame. On one run a fully successful rigorous
construction was abandoned for a naive shortcut the author was presumed, from prose costume
alone, to have taken, and the shortcut was in fact the item's planted model magnet. On the
other, the specification's own standing rule for none-of-the-above menus was scope-narrowed
at commitment by an imported completeness premise the stem never licensed. Both losses were
self-inflicted at the last layer, after the object-level work was already right.

## L69. A completed rigorous route is the referent basis (Q000081)

RIDM 19 surface: Sections 10, 19, and 29. Derivation-first sequencing already licenses an
author-error model only when the rigorous route fails. The missed run exposes the dual
obligation: when the rigorous route succeeds, its result is the referent basis, and an
author model may cap the band but may never displace the completed construction in favor of
a naive shortcut the author is merely presumed to have taken. Prose costume, grammar slips,
flavor framing, casual register, cannot license the displacement; rigor is classified from
checkable structure, and on the missed run the structure (a threshold that binds tightly
only under the geometric frame, a quantization constraint consumed only there) said the
construction was purposeful. In a pool filtered so strong solvers fail, the cleanness of
the naive route's answer is magnet evidence against it, not robustness for it.

## L70. Designed details select the route between completed rivals (Q000087, Q000081)

RIDM 19 surface: Sections 19 and 24. The sprint ran the same decision twice with opposite
correct answers, and the discriminator was the same in both: whose construction the stem's
engineered details pay off under. On the cable run, a given value collapsed to a round
product only inside the curricular image formula, so the formula beat a machine-verified
exact-geometry rival. On the coverage run, the threshold bound tightly only under the
geometric frame, so the geometry beat the additive knapsack. Route selection between
completed candidate routes keys on designed-detail payoff, engineered givens, tightly
binding thresholds, constraints consumed by exactly one frame, and never on rigor ordering
or presumed author sloppiness. A stated requirement that binds tightly under one frame and
hangs slack under the other is a frame discriminator readable before commitment.

## L71. Options rank by falsity before coverage (Q000088)

RIDM 19 surface: Sections 23 and 24. On a which-of-the-following menu without select-all
language, an option containing no false element dominates none-of-the-above and dominates
every option containing a false element, regardless of completeness. Completeness is an
imported premise that requires stem license (select all that apply, the complete set). The
missed run had the measurement layer fully right, every recorded value assigned, the true
indicating pair identified, and lost by choosing none-of-the-above over an incomplete
all-true option. The specification's own generic-truth rule pointed at the keyed answer and
was scope-narrowed away at commitment by a set-completeness reading the stem never stated.
The general form: a standing rule constructed and named by the framework may not be
distinguished away at the decision step by an unlicensed meta-frame; that is charitable
text repair applied to one's own specification.

## L72. Enumerate representation and bookkeeping forks at commitment (Q000089, Q000090)

RIDM 19 surface: Section 30. Two exact-match wins came from committing one answer as an
explicit equivalence class: the systematic form plus named variants, each with its scope.
On a phonetic transcription, the allophonic rendering conventions were bracketed and the
key landed on a named variant. On a path-integral result, the known pair-counting fork
(interaction counted once per pair versus the summand of a full symmetric sum) was
committed with both bookkeepings scoped, and the key held one of them. This is not hedging
between answers; it is one answer under multiple standard representations, and it also
localizes any mismatch to the convention layer for immediate defect differencing. The rule
extends the existing equivalent-forms discipline from named entities and truncation orders
to conventions and bookkeeping generally.

## L73. What worked: witnesses, instruments, sources, honest bands (Q000082 to Q000090)

RIDM 19 surface: Sections 16, 19, 23, 39, and 40. Machine-verified bound-plus-witness
carried the extremal-combinatorics run end to end, with small cases exhausted before any
formula was trusted and the optimal structure read out of search witnesses rather than
remembered. Programmatic assignment of every recorded value, with a constant author-side
offset detected across charge states, made the mass-spectrometry measurement layer exact
even though the run was lost elsewhere. Source identification from distinctive task
vocabulary, followed by re-derivation of the recalled bound, converted a retold item into a
derivation-backed win. A universal-property argument plus an identified named theorem
settled a theory item wholesale. Technique-fingerprint identification with a
mandatory-feature audit carried an image item in a historically hostile category. And the
band ledger behaved: seven of eight high-band commitments hit, the one medium
author-model-backed commitment lost exactly where that class's audit predicts, and the one
low-band commitment that hit was priced low for representation risk that the equivalence
class then absorbed.
