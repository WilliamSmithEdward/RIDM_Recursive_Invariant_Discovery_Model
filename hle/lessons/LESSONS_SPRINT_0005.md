# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0005 (Q000041 to Q000050)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 15, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L41 through L48 are consolidated into RIDM 16, released 2026-08-31.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0006.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: the failures moved one layer downstream. RIDM 15's coverage
and sequencing disciplines worked, and in four of the sprint's six misses the keyed answer
or route was constructed, named, and ranked second. What outranked it each time was a
meta-prior: a coverage-maximization heuristic, a maximal-strictness philosophy, a numerical
coincidence read as design intent, an aesthetically superior interpretation. The reading
that won each time was the canonical one for the item's domain: the standard curricular
chain, the textbook definition of a term of art, the course's taught procedure, the
received cultural association. The ranking layer needs a precedence law. The remaining
misses were a provenance misclassification in the reverse direction (casual costume
concealing a retold experiment) and a checkably defective key marking the referent noise
floor.

## L41. The canonical reading leads the ranking (Q000042; Q000043; Q000044; Q000047; Q000045 positive case)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 19 and 24. When live candidates split between the domain's
canonical reading and a sophisticated alternative backed by meta-inference, the canonical
reading leads: the standard-curriculum drug interaction over a symptom-coverage
maximization, the textbook definition over edge-case skepticism, the course's taught
procedure over a coincidence-driven shortcut, the received symbol association over a
property-mapped construction. Meta-priors about design elegance, item philosophy, or
aesthetic superiority are narrative-tier at ranking time: they may break ties within the
canonical reading and may never overturn it. The one positive application, committing the
canonical editorial route over a cleverer alternative, scored.

## L42. Partition givens into diagnosis layer and decision layer (Q000042)

RIDM 15 surface: Section 23. An authored stem's givens divide by role: scene-setting
givens exist to establish the diagnosis or classification and are consumed by naming it;
the decision layer, often one emphasized constraint or preference clause in conditional
framing, selects the option. Crediting an answer for consuming diagnosis-layer givens
double-counts them and dilutes the decision-layer clause. When the unused-givens audit and
the flagged-constraint audit disagree, the flagged constraint wins.

## L43. Terms of art entail by definition; skepticism belongs to premise scope (Q000043)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 19 and 24. Inside a stated idealized model, terms of art carry
their curricular definitions, and definitional entailments count as must-be-true. Refusing
extrapolation beyond premise scope (unbound futures, unstated environments, unaddressed
behavior) is correct strictness; doubting a textbook definition by importing edge cases the
idealization abstracts away is misplaced strictness and a category error against the
invariant-scope rule.

## L44. Numerology in the givens generates hypotheses, never verdicts (Q000044)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 19, 23, and 24. A discovered numerical near-coincidence among
task givens is narrative-tier: many ratios can be formed from a handful of values, so
near-matches arise by chance, and elevating one to author intent is self-inflicted
multiple-comparisons error. Separately, when an item's canonical route passes through an
instrument the solver lacks (a course chart, a table), the key still follows that route:
reconstruct the instrument, prefer answers near the item's own anchors, and do not switch
to an instrument-free route on determinacy grounds.

## L45. Anti-model traps are retold experiments; hints name their mechanism (Q000046)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 16, 19, and 23. In empirically governed domains, an item that
invites a standard theoretical estimate while hinting at a mechanism is typically retold
from a specific recent experiment whose finding inverts the estimate; casual costume does
not exclude the retold class. A hint of the form "consider X in the analysis" is a flagged
observable: the analysis must give X a causal role with a direction, and a commitment whose
answer would survive deletion of the hint has not consumed it. Idealized closed forms in
such domains rank as inference, not derivation, against source-matched experimental recall.

## L46. A menu mismatch refutes the model, not the menu (Q000046)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 23, 24, and 46. On an authored option menu the intended
computation's result is normally present exactly. A derived value matching no option within
its own uncertainty is evidence against the model choice, not grounds for nearest-option
selection: reopen model identification, looking first for a retold source, before
committing. Noticing the mismatch and proceeding anyway is the undischargeable-alarm
failure by another name.

## L47. Identification-first on research items; anchors and per-part verification earn bands (Q000041; Q000048; Q000049)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 10, 16, 21, and 40. On research-provenance items the bounded
rigorous attempt discharges by identifying the named problem; identification strength, not
recall vividness, sets the band, and recall rises above the isolated-recall floor when
conjecture, construction, and theorem values cohere and an endpoint anchor agrees with an
independently known classical result. Small-parameter instances of a parametric formula
checked against known literature are cheap convergent routes. On multi-part formal items,
classify each part as axiom-trivial, hypothesis-dependent, or refutable before proving
anything; the classification localizes the band, and self-contained axioms outrank any
recognized domain costume in both directions.

## L48. Bands inherit the audit; keys carry a noise floor (Q000042; Q000043; Q000046; Q000047; Q000050)

RIDM 15 surface: Sections 21 and 48. The audited record now places inference-backed
medium commitments far below the medium band's stated meaning, while derivation-backed
medium and high bands track theirs. The medium rigor cap is a ceiling; the audit sets the
floor: inference-backed and author-model commitments default to the low band unless
whole-menu coherence or canonical-reading alignment lifts them. Separately, referent
prediction carries an irreducible noise floor of defective keys; when a key contradicts a
derivation, decompose the surprise, and a defect demonstrated by an explicit classical
counterexample reopens nothing in the reasoning layer, because the revision that would fix
such a miss would corrupt the model.
