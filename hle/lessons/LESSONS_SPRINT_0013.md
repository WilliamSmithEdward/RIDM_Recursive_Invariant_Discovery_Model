# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0013 (Q000121 to Q000130)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 23, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L92 through L97 are consolidated into RIDM 24, released 2026-09-02.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0014.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: seven of ten runs scored, tying the third-best sprint, with
the high band a clean four of four and every derivation-backed commitment a hit. All three
misses sit at the recall and provenance layer, and in each the winning answer was one step
away: a distinct option named as the odd one out and then demoted, a keyed table value
present in the recall set and outranked, a keyed attribute one semantic neighbor from the
recalled one and never generated. Two structural gaps emerge. First, option-provenance
typing exists in the specification but lacked its cheapest trigger: an idea restated
across most of a menu is itself provenance evidence that the cluster was harvested from
the reference population and the outlier is author-voiced. Second, recall has its own
candidate space, conflicting variant values and semantic near-neighbors of a remembered
attribute, and that space needs the same coverage, typed-ranking, and
contamination-pricing discipline the answer layer already has.

## L92. Paraphrase multiplicity marks the harvested cluster (Q000121)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 19 and 23, Control Law 66. A five-option menu held four polished
paraphrases of one diagnosis and one grammatically rough, semantically distinct option.
The cluster structure was noticed and named before ranking, and the distinct option's
keying reading was partially constructed, yet the commitment went into the cluster on a
scope qualifier and a criterion-phrase echo, after the outlier was demoted by an
answer-class argument. The reveal showed the cluster was harvested from model answers and
the outlier was the author's verdict, in the author's own idiolect. The trigger the
discipline lacked: an idea restated across three or more options is the filtered
population's modal answer in harvested form, because a unique-key menu cannot key an
undifferentiated paraphrase; provenance typing by idiolect is mandatory before any
intra-cluster differencing, and on a rough-idiolect stem, register or answer-class
mismatches inside the outlier are the author's pen, not demotion evidence.

## L93. Conflicting recalled values rank by anchor authority (Q000124)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 16, 19, and 20. A dose-table item surfaced three conflicting
recalled values for the keyed cell, including the correct one, each typed as reported. The
commitment went to a reconstruction chained from the framework whose coinage matched the
stem's terminology, partly because the stem's givens collapsed to a round answer under
that variant; the keyed value was the one anchored to the international standard. When
conflicting recalled values for a published cell survive, rank them by the citability and
authority of the source each variant is anchored to, standard over reconstruction, and
treat terminology-lineage arguments as tie-breakers only. A round-number collapse under
one variant is the recall-layer twin of the numerical coincidence: hypothesis fuel, never
a selector.

## L94. Adjacent-canon corroboration is contamination (Q000129)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 16, 21, and 23. A single-attribute recall item was missed by
one semantic neighbor: the committed attribute belonged to the same defiant-symbol family
as the keyed one, and the recall was reinforced by a more famous adjacent work's version
of the motif in the same national tradition. That reinforcement is the availability
magnet operating at the memory layer: when a recalled specific attribute coincides with a
more famous neighboring source's motif, the coincidence is evidence the recall was
contaminated, not corroborated. Enumerate the recalled attribute's semantic
near-neighbors as rivals, require an independent separating probe before committing, and
price unseparated single-noun recall at the low band regardless of how confidently the
title arrives.

## L95. The extent ladder and steric census win forward (Q000126)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 20 and 23, Control Law 65. A product-prediction item was
solved by the disciplines added one sprint earlier, applied in the win direction: the
substitution-extent ladder was enumerated rung by rung, the stated duration and the
stated solubility observable selected the symmetric two-group rung, and the further rung
was refuted by deactivation rather than assumed away. Site selection ran a steric census
before electronic ranking, excluding the electronically best positions on peri-strain
grounds imported from an analogous cationic scaffold. Conditions-select-the-rung
generalizes cleanly from extent of reaction to extent of substitution.

## L96. Rivals are demoted by execution on the stem's own examples (Q000125)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 23 and 40, Control Law 59. On an algorithm-selection menu, the
leading option and its nearest rival differed by one preprocessing step, and the stem's
own worked examples discriminated them: the committed algorithm was verified on the
larger example by constructing the optimal certificate and proving it tight, and the
rival was executed on the same example and produced the wrong count. Executing a rival
option on the stem's own worked instance is the cheapest fully typed demotion available
on such menus, and the stem's examples double as anchor cases for the committed route.

## L97. What worked: criterion-typed givens, exact-form primacy, quiet-filter-first, lattice census (Q000122, Q000123, Q000127, Q000128)

RIDM 23 surface: Sections 11, 23, 30, and 40. Decorative givens were discharged with
typed evidence rather than unease, by checking whether any live reading consumes them
under the asked criterion and whether their own precision markers disqualify them from
supporting an exact answer. An engineered evaluation point that invited an asymptotic
simplification was committed with the exact closed form primary and the approximation
scoped, and the key held the exact form. A norm-threshold event over discrete sums was
collapsed to a point mass by enumerating the reachable value lattice and comparing its
minimal nonzero norm against the threshold before any asymptotics, then verified by
exact enumeration. A conjunctive-criterion menu was solved by typing every option
against the quiet filter before ranking on the vivid one, exposing the designed trap
whose vivid-filter champion fails the quiet filter.
