# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0004 (Q000031 to Q000040)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 14, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L33 through L40 are consolidated into RIDM 15, released 2026-08-31.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0005.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: RIDM 14's criterion-referent discipline, introduced after
sprint 0003, was powerful but uncalibrated. Three of the four misses were author-model
failures in opposite directions: one attributed error to a rigorous author and substituted
an author-model for a feasible derivation, one attributed depth to a shallow author and
repaired the item's text into a sophistication its key never had, and one modeled the right
author at the right depth but with the wrong chain. The author-model needs sequencing
(derivation first), a competence estimate grounded in observable tells, and rigor-matched
ranking criteria. The hits show the same referent machinery working when gated correctly.

## L33. Derivation first; author-models only after the rigorous route fails (Q000031, incorrect answer)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 10, 19, and 26. Declaring the rigorous route infeasible is itself
a decisive claim requiring a bounded attempt as evidence; a special-function closed form is
still an exact-matchable key. Rigor classification must rest on the item's checkable
mathematical structure, not its prose register: generated-sounding framing around a
well-posed computation is presentation. The author-error model may substitute for
derivation only after the derivation has actually failed, produced nothing matchable, or
exceeded a stated budget.

## L34. Author competence is an estimated parameter; rank chains by rigor-matched criteria (Q000037; Q000031; Q000039 positive case)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 19 and 21. The two author-model misses bracketed the same
parameter from opposite sides. Estimate author competence from observable tells (vacuous
conditions, undefined coinages, redundant constraints, and the authoring population's known
review standards) before ranking candidate keys. On incoherent items, prefer the shallowest
arithmetic chain requiring the fewest repairs of the author's text; a reading that must
repair the text (turning a vacuous condition into a substantive one) is penalized, not
rewarded, because the author who needed the repair never made it. Reserve
every-condition-is-purposeful ranking for items whose conditions already read as purposeful.
Population priors matter: on an expert-reviewed benchmark, keys that make the item pointless
or indefensible carry low prior even when the surface suggests a naive author.

## L35. Audit absent mandatory features and forbid stem enrichment (Q000034, incorrect answer)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 15 and 23. The unused-given audit has a dual: candidate
diagnoses or readings whose canonical, vignette-mandatory features are absent from the stem
lose standing, and on authored items the withheld feature is often the intended
discriminator. Separately, filling in an unstated fact from a plausible association (a
comorbidity implying an exposure history) enters an assumed claim into the chain as if the
stem had reported it; type it as assumed and re-rank without it. Prefer readings that
consume a given causally over readings that merely correlate with it.

## L36. The comparison is part of the proposition; source identification gates retold commitments (Q000032, incorrect answer; Q000036 positive case)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 16, 19, and 20. Recalled evidence carries a contrast type
(case versus control, within-population, dose response); a direction attested under one
contrast is narrative-tier for an item whose population defines another, and can invert.
On retold items, spend the first probe identifying the source from the stem's phrasing:
when the fingerprint uniquely selects a source whose central claim maps to one option, the
commitment earns a real band; when the source cannot be identified, the structural prior
binds regardless of how available a generic direction feels. Verbatim lexical match to the
likely source's wording discriminates between paraphrase-duplicate options.

## L37. Encodings have convention spaces (Q000033)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 23 and 30. Any encoding task carries implementation conventions
invisible in the statement (what consumes a key position, where counting starts). A
partially coherent decode localizes the defect to the convention layer rather than the key
layer; enumerate conventions the way candidate readings are enumerated. Staged puzzles
validate forward: each stage's output earning coherence at the next stage upgrades both
stages' support.

## L38. Whole-menu coherence is the strong form of recall validation (Q000035)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 16 and 24. Recall that must jointly explain the status of every
option under one consistent rule set with no leftovers earns a band that isolated
single-fact recall does not. Entity names in authored items are chosen exactly; a national
suffix or a parent-versus-subsidiary form is a distinct fact, and near-forms are
discriminators. A vivid detail duplicated across options discriminates nothing and exists
to tempt an invented rule; name the rule it tempts and check whether it exists.

## L39. None-of-the-above is refuted by any surviving true option (Q000038)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 23 and 24. Evaluate multi-clause options clause by clause
against the given data, kill any option with one false clause, and evaluate the none option
last: it is refuted by any option whose truth is decidable from the given evidence, however
generic. Under a choose-the-correct-answer criterion, scope-humble truths dominate; an
author keying none-of-the-above does not leave a true statement in the menu.

## L40. In named-template items, the deviation is the question (Q000039; Q000040)

RIDM 14 surface: Sections 23 and 30. When a stimulus instantiates a famous template
(a named reaction, a classic problem), audit its deviations from the canonical recipe
first; a swapped reagent or altered condition is a flagged observable, and the planted
template answer is the almost-right invariant. For exact-match commitments with multiple
equivalent representations, state the systematic form and the likely trivial form the
author used. A contract reading that trivializes a stated precision requirement is
disfavored by coherence, and on retold quantitative items, agreement between the fresh
derivation and the source's published value is the strongest available composite support.
