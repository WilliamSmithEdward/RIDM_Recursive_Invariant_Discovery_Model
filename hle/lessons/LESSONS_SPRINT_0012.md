## L86. The pool's filter is instance evidence (Q000115, validated on Q000117)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 19, 21, and 24. A yes/no foundations item sat on a genuine
scholarly split, with defensible literature on both sides. Both verdicts were constructed
with their keying readings, and the survival-filter argument was generated pre-commitment:
frontier models answer the consensus verdict, so the item survives an adversarially
filtered pool only if the key sits on the other side. That argument was then demoted by a
population prior about expert reviewers avoiding contested positions, and the key held the
other side. The filter that admitted an item into its pool is part of the item's
provenance: on split-verdict items, predict the filtered population's modal answer and
treat the item's survival as typed instance evidence against it. A population prior about
authors or reviewers is narrative-tier against that signal, and the consensus verdict is
the planted magnet precisely because the consensus is what the filtered models reproduce.
Two runs later the same discipline, applied deliberately to a famous-demonstration item
whose stem quietly moved one element of the canonical setup, selected the trap-inverted
reading and scored.

## L87. Conditions select the rung of an extent ladder (Q000119)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 16, 20, and 23, Control Law 59. A stepwise-transformation item
(sequential ring closures toward a famous fully-cyclized endpoint) was answered with the
endpoint, although the stem's conditions were mild and dilute. The partial-extent rival
was named mid-analysis and demoted with an untyped duration phrase ("long enough to go to
completion"), and the half-surfaced recall that the endpoint's canonical preparation
requires far harsher conditions was averaged away as a doubt. On telescoping or stepwise
processes, the candidate space is the extent-of-reaction ladder, one candidate per rung;
the stated conditions' harshness selects the rung; prominently mild or dilute stated
conditions are an extent-limiter flag, not a testament to the substrate's reactivity; and
a surfaced recall that the endpoint's canonical conditions exceed the stem's is typed
refutation of the endpoint under the conflicting-evidence rules, not a hedge. Duration
and effort phrases are untyped demotions wherever constructed-reading demotion applies.

## L88. Precision mismatch predicts referent noise (Q000114)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 11, 21, and 48. A computation item supplied one parameter only
as an order of magnitude while demanding a three-decimal answer. Coherence correctly
forced the literal reading (the only exact value the stem supports), the no-effect
shortcut was computed pre-reveal, and the commitment was banded medium. The key proved
internally inconsistent three ways, each reproduced exactly: its rationale used a
different exponent than its own stem, its rationale's final number equals the pre-computed
no-effect shortcut, and its answer field is reachable only by additionally violating the
stem's own rounding instruction. The general rule: a stem that pairs an
order-of-magnitude given with an exact-precision demand carries a criterion-information
mismatch inside its own text, and that mismatch is a predictor of author implementation
inconsistency; band down for referent noise even after coherence pins the literal
reading, and always compute the parameter-free variant.

## L89. Options carry provenance, typed by idiolect (Q000113)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 19 and 23. A count-the-correct-statements item mixed four
skeptical critiques with one affirmative interpretation of an author-presented theorem.
The mathematics settled three critiques as unfounded; the contest was a literally-true
critique of a proposition the claim never asserts. The discriminating instrument was
provenance typing: the affirmative option shares the stem's distinctive idiolect, marking
it author-voiced, while the critiques read as harvested model outputs planted to be
dismissed, and an author who wrote the affirmative gloss would not key the critique that
contradicts it. On statement-evaluation menus, type each option's likely authorship by
matching idiolect against the stem before ranking; an author-voiced option carries the
author's verdict, and a true-but-tangential critique is keyed by the author's reading of
it, not by its truth value. This extends the stem-as-style-corpus discipline from
construction slots to option provenance.

## L90. Menu architecture identifies the frame (Q000111)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 23 and 24. A causative-organism vignette offered a menu that
omitted the default diagnosis's canonical organism while containing a rival diagnosis's
canonical organism plus two name-association traps, and the stem's host profile was
consumed causally only under the rival diagnosis. Menu-mismatch reopening run in reverse
is frame identification: a menu that cannot be keyed coherently under the default frame,
but partitions cleanly under a rival frame (key present, traps explicable, default's
obvious answer absent), is architectural evidence for the rival frame before any option
is ranked. The absence of the default frame's obvious answer is evidence about the
intended frame, not a menu defect.

## L91. What worked: trace counting, slot decoupling, pattern differencing, per-edge anchors (Q000112, Q000116, Q000118, Q000120)

RIDM 22 surface: Sections 16, 19, 23, 30, and 40. A best-decay item was derived by
counting which boundary traces the evolution preserves, fixing the generic spectral
coefficient decay and hence the optimal algebraic exponent, cross-checked by
differentiating the decay law through the coupling equation; the key's boundary-layer
route reaches the same exponent in different coordinates. A compound
identification-plus-verdict item was split by robustness partitioning: the verdict slot
was invariant across the credible identification set, decoupling it from the
identification band. A missing-reagent scheme was solved by protection-pattern
differencing, each rival reagent predicting a distinct observable protection state in
the drawn product, so the drawing itself refuted the rivals. A path-sign vector was
assembled slot by slot from per-edge literature anchors, with whole-story coherence used
as a check rather than a generator, and the key's rationale matched every slot's stated
mechanism. Wins in this sprint were carried by instruments that convert referent
prediction into observable differencing.
