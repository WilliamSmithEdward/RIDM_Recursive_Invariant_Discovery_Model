# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0014 (Q000131 to Q000140)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 26, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L98 through L103 are consolidated into RIDM 27, released 2026-09-02.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0015.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: four of ten runs scored, the weakest sprint since 0006, but
the six misses share one root to an unusual degree. In five of them a selection layer, a
bookkeeping convention, a candidate model, a recall variant, a chain ranking, was resolved
by the effect the choice had on the ranking rather than canonically and blind to
consequences: a power-flow model kept because it discriminated among options, a spectral
counting convention kept because it rescued the leading candidate, first-generation
candidates displaced by cleverer-feeling rivals, a rival chain demoted because its
formula use was epidemiologically impure, a keying reading demoted because the field's
standard gloss disagreed. The complementary signal is about whose correctness governs:
on authored referents, domain-internal rigor repeatedly outranked author-fit evidence at
the ranking step, exactly where the specification says the author's implementation is
the operative referent. Coverage stayed strong (eight of ten keyed answers entered the
candidate set); the losses were demotion-side.

## L98. Selection layers are resolved blind (Q000132, Q000133, Q000137)

RIDM 26 surface: Sections 20 and 24, Control Law 20. Three misses shared one move: a
convention, model, or variant was selected after observing what the selection did to the
ranking. A candidate power-factor bookkeeping was adopted because it was the only one
that separated the options; a spectral counting convention was adopted because it kept
the leading skeleton alive; a derived title and a plain namesake reading were displaced
by later rivals whose sole advantage was feeling more designed. Discriminative power,
candidate rescue, and designedness are consequences, not evidence. Resolve every
selection layer canonically first, then let the resolved layer rank the candidates; a
selection justified by its effect on the outcome is manufactured evidence wherever it
appears.

## L99. Generated items key on the seed (Q000132)

RIDM 26 surface: Sections 19 and 24, Control Laws 17 and 18. On a structurally
incoherent generated item, the key is the generator's own output, and its best
predictors are internal: centrality in the distractor cloud, where foils read as
perturbations of one option, and anchoring to stated givens. The pool-filter signal
collapses to narrative tier there, because reference models scatter on incoherent stems
and their modal answer stops being predictable. The filter's strength is conditioned on
the item's rigor class: decisive on expert-authored split verdicts, weak on generated
noise.

## L100. Author-fit outranks domain rigor on authored referents (Q000133, Q000138, Q000139)

RIDM 26 surface: Sections 16, 19, and 20, Control Law 18. Three misses graded candidate
chains by domain-internal correctness where the decisive claim was a prediction of the
author's implementation. A school-canon item's clean ring-opening was demoted with
research-grade selectivity data; a field-standard causal gloss was recalled to overrule
an expert author's sharper point; a screening-formula chain was demoted because it
transplants an attack rate into a slot that canonically takes a different quantity. On
curriculum-canon items, feasibility and selectivity are judged by what the curriculum
teaches. A because-claim is verified by toggling the claimed factor in a constructed
variant, never by quoting the field's gloss. And a domain-correctness objection that
describes a predictable author transplant is evidence for that chain as the authored
referent, not against it.

## L101. The filter check runs per statement (Q000138)

RIDM 26 surface: Sections 19 and 24, Control Law 17. On a multi-select menu, each
statement has its own reference-population modal answer, so the pool-filter discipline
fires per statement, not once per item. A statement that restates the field's standard
account is the consensus magnet in statement form, and an expert item's survival in a
filtered pool is typed evidence that at least one such statement is keyed against the
gloss.

## L102. Consumption extends to symbols and announced inventories (Q000136, Q000139, Q000133)

RIDM 26 surface: Section 23, Control Law 15. Three consumption gaps, each detectable
before commitment. A formula-answer item introduces its symbols deliberately: a
prominently typed parameter that no live candidate consumes marks a missing candidate
family, the symbol-level form of the unused-given audit. A stem that counts its own
evidence, announcing how many numbers were obtained, sets a consumption contract: a
chain leaving part of the announced inventory idle loses to a completed rival consuming
all of it, and scene-setting discharge is unavailable for counted givens. And a
qualifier paired with an observable, heating alongside gas release, is a mechanism hint
under the deletion test, committing only readings that give it causal force.

## L103. First-generation candidates are protected (Q000136, Q000137)

RIDM 26 surface: Sections 16 and 23, Control Laws 19 and 20. Two misses lost candidates
that were generated early from the plainest evidence: one brainstormed formula was
dropped unprobed and turned out keyed; two plain sub-answers were displaced by
thematically cleverer rivals and both were keyed. A candidate produced in the first
pass from the strongest plain evidence is displaced only by typed evidence at matching
scope, and every brainstormed candidate is either probed to a verdict or explicitly
discharged before ranking. Silent drops are coverage failures the gate should catch.
