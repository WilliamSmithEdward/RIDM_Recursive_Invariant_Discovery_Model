Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 32, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L124 through L128 are consolidated into RIDM 33, released 2026-09-08.

keyed answer entered the candidate set on seven of ten runs. The hits were a bound-and-witness

## L124. Enumerate at the stem's own givens, and the deflated detail is not the mechanism (Q000182)

RIDM 32 surface: Sections 23 and 24, Control Laws 15 and 22. A casual puzzle handed a
software engineer a short word in another script and said the place was on the map but
unlabelled. The encoding family was generated, computed in two uniform-case variants that
produced nothing coordinate-shaped, and set aside; the stem's own casing, a capital first
letter and lowercase remainder, was never run, and it is the variant whose character codes
form an in-range coordinate pair. The commitment was then built on the stem's pun, which the
stem itself deflates when a character laughs it off, and the plain route that the stated
profession implies was displaced by a chain of four inferences whose designedness was read
as support. Fold: a transformation or encoding family is probed first at the stem's own
case, spelling, and character inventory, and a family discharged on variants that alter a
given was never probed; the mechanism-naming hint is the detail whose deletion kills the
route, a stated profession, instrument, or capability, while a detail the stem itself
deflates is scene-setting and a route built on it survives the hint's deletion.

## L125. Register is not rigor, and the filter needs a converged elimination (Q000184)

RIDM 32 surface: Sections 19, 23, and 24, Control Laws 17 and 18. A one-line stem in
non-native English with five procedural options was typed generated-class from its grammar.
The class struck the clause test and licensed seed centrality, which selected the option
whose extra clause names a measure that does not measure the asked quantity; the first
instinct, the option carrying a practitioner's control for a confound, was declared the
population's modal answer by assumption and demoted by the pool filter as a tie-break. The
key was the first instinct, with a rationale written by a practitioner who knows the design.
None of the generated class's structural tells was present. Fold: framing and register,
awkward or non-native grammar included, are presentation, and the generated class is
assigned only on a structural tell; a stem fluent in its content under a rough surface is
casual expert, with the clause test licensed and centrality not. And the filter fires only
against a converged elimination, the option the licensed instrument settles on; a first
instinct is not an elimination, and a filter used to move off an instinct is a tie-break at a
strength the item never earned.

## L126. A truncated stem keys the source's terminal quantity (Q000187)

RIDM 32 surface: Sections 19, 21, and 30, Control Laws 24 and 26. A textbook exercise
arrived with a preamble about energy transfer and a last sentence asking for the flow
vector. The vector was derived in both regions, checked by integrating it to the rate at
which the field works on the enclosed charge, and committed alone. The key is that
integrated rate per unit length, the source's final answer, with a rationale that computes
the committed vector on the way. The framework prices transcribed numbers and degraded
implementations as referent noise; a stem cut short of a multi-part source, keyed at the
source's terminal quantity, is a further case in the family and was not priced, although the
terminal quantity was in hand. Fold: a retold stem whose preamble frames a terminal quantity
while its last sentence asks an intermediate one is keyed at the terminal quantity as often
as at the asked one; when the two lie one step apart, both are stated as scoped
representations of one claim, which prices the truncation referent at no cost.

## L127. Unreachable keys: lifted parameters and illustrative lists (Q000185, Q000190)

RIDM 32 surface: Sections 21 and 23, Control Laws 15 and 24. Two low-band misses with no
stem-derivable route to the key. In the first, a fragment lifted from a worked example
asked for an expression that is one side of the author's boundary equation; the stem states
one right-hand side and the key was computed with another, and no reading of the stem
produces the keyed number. In the second, a generated stem asked for a minimum ratio whose
numerator and denominator it never counts; the only numbers in the stem were three words
after "e.g.", which were promoted to a feature count, and the key rests on eight features the
generator invented. Fold: a lifted example's stated parameter can differ from the parameter
its key was computed with, an allowance that is named, cannot be priced to a value, and
keeps the band low with the commitment at the stem's number; and an illustrative list is
presentation, never an inventory, so no count is consumed that the stem does not state as a
count, and a generated ratio with no stated count is a low-band guess at the generator's
roundest fraction with only the answer's class claimed.

## L128. Given fidelity is a coverage layer (sprint synthesis)

RIDM 32 surface: Sections 2, 23, and 29, Control Law 15. Earlier sprints lost items at
ranking, by demoting a keyed candidate already in hand. This sprint's recoverable misses
were lost before ranking, at the point where the stem's givens are turned into candidates:
the givens' case, the count a list does or does not state, the quantity the criterion
sentence asks against the quantity the preamble frames, and the class the surface suggests
against the class the structure licenses. Each is a fidelity failure between the stem as
written and the stem as enumerated. The fold needs no new law: the consumption discipline
runs to the character level, the rigor class is read from structure with register
discounted, the asked and framed quantities are both carried when they differ, and the
commitment gate's consumption check names all four.
