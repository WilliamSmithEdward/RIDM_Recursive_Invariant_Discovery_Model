# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0018 (Q000171 to Q000180)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 31, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L119 through L123 are consolidated into RIDM 32, released 2026-09-08.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0019.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: six of ten runs scored, the high band two of two, and the
keyed answer entered the candidate set on eight of ten runs. The hits were bound-and-witness
derivation, convergent hand and library routes, a verified theorem hypothesis, an exact
subset dynamic program with both readings computed, an intersection-solved trivia chain, and
one filter-led menu win at the low band. Of the four misses, one was against a checkably
defective generated key whose every exponent was miscomputed. The other three share one
family with the previous sprints' misses, author-fit displaced by the solver's own
sophistication, and they extend it to three referent objects the framework had not named:
the author's search space, the author's chart, and the author's diagnosis. A global optimum
reached by rewriting the stated expression displaced the catalogue composition the author
priced; a hand-built model of a solver's output displaced the recalled chart value the
author read off; and a textbook mechanism, chosen over the diagnosis the author's bench
experience blames, was committed after the pool-filter verdict against it had been set
aside on a population split that was never constructed. One item was skipped without a run
number when the harness safeguard blocked the reply.

## L119. An extremal claim is the author's optimum over the author's search space (Q000171)

RIDM 31 surface: Sections 19 and 23, Control Laws 18 and 22. A casually authored item asked
for the smallest cost of a computation in a public catalogue's cost idiom. The rigorous
route found a cheaper decomposition than any catalogue composition and committed it; the
key composed catalogue entries in the shape of the stated expression, one entry per stated
coefficient, and summed their published counts. The framework says the referent is the
author's implementation; it did not say that a smallest, largest, or best is the author's
optimum over the space the author searched, or that the stem's idiom fingerprints that
space. The author's route was in the candidate set and was costed on a recalled absence,
that the catalogue lacked one entry, which is a reported claim used as if verified. Fold:
extremal claims are the author's optimum over the search space the stem's idiom
fingerprints, the composition mirroring the stated expression is the primary referent
candidate with the rewritten global optimum a priced rival, and a recalled absence of a
catalogue, table, or library entry demotes nothing until probed.

## L120. Boundary questions and modeled adjustments to recall (Q000173)

RIDM 31 surface: Sections 16 and 24, Control Laws 16 and 20. A one-line recall item asked
for the worst member of a set that a solver-derived chart admits. Recall of the standard
charts put the cutoff inside the set, and that plain first-pass candidate was displaced by
an inference: a hand-built equity sketch argued that a stated game condition lifts the
whole set into the range. Two rules were already in place and were not applied. The
framework says a derivation outranks recall only when its governing model is supported,
and a sketch of what a solver would output is not a supported model of the solver. And the
question's own form was an alarm: asking for the boundary element presupposes that the
boundary lies inside the set, so a reading under which the whole set qualifies empties the
question, and it was noticed and dismissed as a possible trick. Fold: a question that asks
for the boundary element of a set is refuted, by its own emptiness, under any reading in
which the whole set qualifies unless the stem licenses the trivial case; and an adjustment
to a recalled empirical value argued from a hand-built model of the instrument that
produced it is an inference at the decision step, pricing a rival and capping the band,
never displacing the recalled value.

## L121. A generated numeric chain opens at low (Q000175)

RIDM 31 surface: Sections 19 and 21, Control Law 24. A well-formed Boltzmann computation
carrying generated-item tells was derived exactly, checked under three constant roundings,
and committed at medium with the componentry allowance named. The key miscomputed every
exponent by a different factor, so no priced variant could reach it, and it passed the
value-class check because its components still summed to the stated total. The
componentry allowance prices a slipped step; it cannot price a chain in which each step
slipped independently, which is what a generated rationale's arithmetic looks like. Fold:
a generated item whose key is a multi-step numeric chain opens at low, because its
referent's error surface is arithmetic noise that no variant enumeration prices; the
value-class check still runs, and strict grading still applies.

## L122. The filter verdict yields only to a constructed rival route (Q000180)

RIDM 31 surface: Sections 19 and 24, Control Laws 17 and 18. A troubleshooting item stated
every condition of a procedure and offered one fix per condition. The attempt's own
elimination converged on one option, which the framework already names as the reference
population's modal answer with the item's survival keyed against it. The verdict was then
declared indeterminate on the hypothesis that the population would split, with no rival
modal route constructed, and the modal option was committed. The key was the fix the
author's bench diagnosis blames, a selectivity failure fingerprinted by a substrate with two
reactive sites, while the attempt fingerprinted the diagnosis by the stem's most
conspicuous number, which on a stem where every condition maps to an option discriminates
nothing. Fold: a fired filter verdict against the solver's own converged elimination is
overridden only by a constructed rival modal route of comparable pull, and a hypothesized
split is alarm dismissal; on a troubleshooting stem the author's fix targets the condition
their diagnosis blames, and the diagnosis is fingerprinted by the setup's structure.

## L123. The referent has three objects (sprint synthesis)

RIDM 31 surface: Sections 2 and 19, Control Law 18. Three misses in one family: the
framework models the author as the implementer of a procedure, and the misses were on
items where the graded claim was not a procedure's output but an optimum, a chart reading,
and a diagnosis. In each the solver's route was better by the domain's standards and wrong
as a prediction of the key, which is the same author-fit-over-domain-rigor mechanism the
framework already corrects for implementations. The fold needs no new law: the referent
paragraph names the author's search space and diagnosis beside the author's
implementation, the stem's idiom and structure are the fingerprints that identify them,
and the plain first-pass candidate's protection extends to the recalled chart value and
the catalogue-shaped composition.
