# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0008 (Q000071 to Q000080)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 18, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L62 through L68 are consolidated into RIDM 19, released 2026-09-01.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0009.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: the derivation and instrumentation layers are strong, and the
remaining losses concentrate in referent prediction, predicting what an authored key holds
when the item's author reasons in a different community, style, or frame than the solver.
Six of ten runs scored. The four misses were: a rival demoted on an assumed claim that no
supporting gloss exists when the author's own field supplied one; a checkably defective key
that executed the exact canonical error its item's theorem exists to correct; a frame choice
where a discovered error cancellation was read as designed intent and overturned the plainer
data-faithful frame; and an author-program accounting slot whose keyed variant, built from
the author's own demonstrated idioms, never entered the candidate set. One additional item
was voided mid-sprint by a reveal that fired before the answer was committed, the second
such protocol failure across sprints, this time surviving a specification-level law against
it.

## L62. Absence of a gloss is an assumed universal (Q000072)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 23 and 24. A live rival may be demoted only on typed evidence. A
refutation of the form no coherent author could read option X as Y is an absence claim, an
assumed universal, and it enters the ledger as assumed, not observed. The probe it requires
is a search of the item's own provenance community for the gloss: on the missed run the
gloss existed, published and citable, in the author's field. Until that probe runs, the
rival stays live and the menu-coherence argument built on its absence is unsupported.

## L63. Announced domain transfers re-index the canon (Q000072)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 19, 21, and 24. When a stem announces that a concept usually
studied in one field is being applied in another, the operative canon is the destination
field's, the community the author works in and grades from, not the source field imported
for framing. A directional effect that is textbook in the source field can invert in the
destination field's literature. Whole-menu coherence built on a canon choice inherits that
choice's uncertainty and cannot lift the band above the canon claim's own support; in pools
filtered so frontier models fail, the cleanest inversion under the imported canon is the
model magnet, and its very cleanness is evidence of the trap.

## L64. A named canonical trap predicts key defects (Q000073)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 21 and 48. When the correct route passes an error mode with a
name, the naive shortcut a standard theorem exists to correct, and that shortcut also
produces a clean closed form, the shortcut's output is a live defective-key candidate on an
otherwise well-posed item. Compute it alongside the true result before reveal, so post-hoc
defect differencing is immediate, and apply the compound-answer allowance to the band
instead of committing at full derivation strength. Derivation-first sequencing still forbids
committing the shortcut itself while the rigorous route succeeds; the lesson prices the
referent risk, it does not move the answer.

## L65. Estimation licenses the incomputable, not the givens (Q000075)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 23 and 24. On a calculation over given data, the canonical frame
represents the givens exactly at the item's own precision and spends the error budget on
constants that cannot be represented. A route that perturbs given data to optimize a
secondary objective is a rival requiring explicit license from the stem. A discovered
cancellation, coincidence, or forced-move chain that makes the sophisticated route feel
designed is a hypothesis generator about the author, never a frame discriminator: the same
evidence is equally consistent with an author who never left the plain frame. This is
sophistication capture operating at the frame layer, one level above the reading layer the
canonical-precedence law already governs.

## L66. The stem's examples are the author's style corpus (Q000080)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 19 and 23. When a slot grades the author's own construction, a
program, an accounting, a recipe, the item's example code and prose are evidence of the
author's idioms and belong in the candidate generation step. Enumerate the demonstrated
habits, uniform typing, variable reuse, results computed in expressions, as first-class
variants before ranking any reconstruction. A modal guess over solver-natural variants is
coverage myopia on the style axis: the ranking was careful over a set that never contained
the key, and the keyed construction may not even be optimal, confirming the slot is a
referent prediction, not a derivation.

## L67. Sequencing laws need mechanical interlocks (process)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 35 and 48. The oracle-sequencing law survived one sprint on
discipline alone and then failed operationally again: a reveal fired before commitment
during a harness disruption, and the item was voided under the law's own remedy. A
sequencing obligation enforced only by procedure fails under operational noise, so where the
harness permits it, the commitment must be encoded into the oracle-consulting action itself,
making premature consultation structurally impossible rather than merely prohibited. The
evaluation harness for this project now requires a recorded commitment before it will
reveal a key, and the law generalizes: an admission precondition worth stating is worth
enforcing in the mechanism that executes the action.

## L68. What worked: consumption, witnesses, instruments, honest lows (Q000071 to Q000079)

RIDM 18 surface: Sections 16, 21, 23, 39, and 40. Treating tight stated ranges as granted
capabilities that must be consumed discriminated between rival keys on the memory-golf run.
Construction load-bearingness selected the cylinder-count reading on the cycle-budget run
against the bare-window model magnet, the exact anti-model direction opposite to the
canon-inversion trap two runs earlier, confirming the invariant is consuming the stem's
construction, not modeling other models. Bound-plus-witness pairs carried both the
memory-golf optimum and the incidence-geometry exponent, with the witness halving the
recall exposure on the latter. Instrumented observation, programmatic translation and
motif scanning, multi-precision stability sweeps, upgraded decisive claims to observed on
the sequence and evaluation runs. Component-wise double derivation put the dressed
integral exactly on its menu option and made the defective-key offset proof immediate on
the gradient run. And the two low-band commitments both lost, which is what a calibrated
low band is for; the honest spread pricing on wide referent distributions should be kept
against future pressure to inflate.
