# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0015 (Q000141 to Q000150)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 27, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L104 through L109 are consolidated into RIDM 28, released 2026-09-04.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0016.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: four of ten runs scored, with the high band three for three
and every derivation-backed hit instrument-led (an exact optimization with a certificate,
a support-function derivation replicated numerically, an image authenticity test by
enlarged crops and metadata). Of the six misses, two ran against keys with checkable
defects, and four share one root that sprint 0014 already named from the other side: a
rule the framework states reached the keyed verdict before commitment and was then
overridden by a meta-level argument, an author-population prior, a designedness reading,
a trap-design intuition, an elegance preference. The rules were present; the gate's
standing-rules check did not make their verdicts bind. The remaining losses were coverage
at the convention and author-execution layers, where a variant family was enumerated
incompletely, plus one instrument built without an anchor case.

## L104. Fired verdicts bind: the verdict ledger (Q000141, Q000143, Q000147, Q000148)

RIDM 27 surface: Sections 24 and 29, Control Laws 20 and 26. In four misses a standing
rule fired and pointed at the key: the canonical capability criterion, the decisive
pool-filter signal, the transplant objection that switches sides, the deletion test on a
qualifier, the harvested-cluster outlier, the strict reading of a ceiling operator.
Each was then overridden at commitment by an argument of a kind the framework already
demotes to tie-breaking. The standing-rules check has to be executable: before
commitment, list every rule that reached a verdict on the item, with its verdict; a
commitment contrary to a fired verdict needs typed evidence at matching scope, and a
meta-prior, a coincidence, or a designedness reading is never that evidence. Three rules
reaching one verdict and none binding is a gate execution failure, not a coverage one.

## L105. The rigor class licenses the menu instrument (Q000147, Q000141)

RIDM 27 surface: Sections 19 and 23, Control Laws 17 and 18. Seed centrality predicts
the key on generated menus, where distractors are perturbations of the generator's own
output. On retold and expert menus the author perturbs away from the key, so the
features shared across options mark the harvested cluster and the semantically distinct
outlier is presumptively the key. The framework held both rules; a run typed an item
retold and then applied the generated-item rule. The rigor class is resolved first and
selects the instrument, and on every expert menu the textbook-consensus option is the
magnet the pool-filter check predicts against, so that check runs as a required step.

## L106. A coincidence that holds under every candidate discriminates nothing (Q000148, Q000141)

RIDM 27 surface: Section 24, Control Law 20. An integrality argument selected a boundary
convention although every enumerated variant produced an integer threshold, because the
givens were powers of two; a halide named in an example selected a reading although it
was an ordinary example under the rival. A designed detail is one that binds under one
candidate and hangs slack under another; a feature present under all of them is noise.
Boundary conventions resolve canonically from the operator the formula uses, a ceiling
making its threshold exclusive, never from the roundness of the threshold.

## L107. Formula-plug items are keyed by the author's variant of the formula (Q000148)

RIDM 27 surface: Section 23, Control Laws 14 and 21. A level-count formula was taken
from one paper's cost model and three variants of it were priced; the key used a fourth
member of the same family, a buffer-inclusive cumulative capacity with an additive
constant inside the logarithm. On a formula-plug item, enumerate the family's published
variants, including additive-constant and normalization forms, before ranking, and
price them as convention forks.

## L108. Chain execution is componentry (Q000150)

RIDM 27 surface: Sections 19 and 21, Control Law 24. A five-step casual chain matched
the key through its fourth step and lost at the fifth, where the author read a lowercase
glyph as its uppercase look-alike and summed the distinct letters. On a multi-step
casual chain, the author's plausible executions of each step, glyph confusions, set
against multiset readings, case handling, are priced variants, and the compound answer's
band is the weakest step's, which for such chains is low.

## L109. A self-built instrument is anchored before the live case (Q000149)

RIDM 27 surface: Sections 39 and 40, Control Law 24. A disc classifier for wild
semistable reduction was built during the run and trusted on the unknown curve at the
medium band with two known blind spots argued away. The closed-world rule already asks
for an anchor case; for an instrument constructed inside the task the anchor is
mandatory, run before the live case on a curve with an independently known outcome, and
the band cannot exceed what the anchor supports. The key on that item was itself
defective, which changes the grade not at all and the lesson not at all.
