# Framework Lessons - Sprint 0006 (Q000051 to Q000060)

Distilled, content-free lessons from the sprint's runs in [../runs/](../runs/). Each lesson
names the run that produced it, the RIDM surface it touches, and the change it suggests. One
lessons file exists per sprint of ten runs.

Section numbers cited in this file and in this sprint's run logs refer to RIDM 16, the
specification version under evaluation during the sprint.

Consolidation status: L49 through L56 are consolidated into RIDM 17, released 2026-08-31.
The next sprint's lessons start in `lessons/LESSONS_SPRINT_0007.md`.

The sprint's dominant signal: the residual moved up another layer. Sprint 0005 exposed
ranking among readings; this sprint exposed selection of the governing frame, the
convention, ruleset, terminological canon, or enumeration scope inside which readings are
even formed. Three genuine misses were frame-selection failures: a canon indexed to the
wrong population, a silently imported rule variant whose rival would have made the stem's
elaborate construction load-bearing, and a stated scope shrunk by imported domain rigor.
Two more were a standing-structure census skip and an unstated ensemble prior overriding
per-slot evidence. Two further strict misses were graded against keys with demonstrable
internal defects, both established by reproducing the key's number exactly from the correct
value plus an identified error mechanism; the referent noise floor is real and concentrates
in multi-component hand-computed keys.

## L49. The governing frame is a candidate, not background (Q000051; Q000059; Q000056)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 19, 23, and 24. An imported convention, a terminological canon,
a ruleset variant, an enumeration boundary, is a candidate reading that must be enumerated
and priced, never a background fact. Two selectors rank frames: provenance, the canon of
the item's source community at its most specific (a cited paper's own usage outranks the
discipline's textbook default), and load-bearingness, preferring the frame under which the
stem's elaborate construction does work. A frame that renders a detailed constructed
position decorative fails coherence exactly as a reading that trivializes a stated
constraint.

## L50. Do not impoverish the stem (Q000056)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 15 and 23. The dual of stem enrichment: shrinking a stated scope
("all possible") with an unstated domain premise is an assumed claim doing silent work. Run
the deletion test on the stem's own scope words; if the committed answer would be unchanged
with the scope phrase removed, the phrase was not consumed and the permissive enumeration
leads.

## L51. Alarms discharge or bind (Q000059; Q000056)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 24 and 29. Noticing an anomaly and proceeding is a recurring
failure despite the rule existing. The discipline must be mechanical: every anomaly noted
during analysis enters the commitment gate, where it either discharges, meaning the
committed reading explains it, or binds, capping the band and forcing a re-rank against the
rival that would explain it. A noted-and-dismissed alarm is a gate failure, not a footnote.

## L52. Census the standing structures before evaluating candidates (Q000058)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 23 and 29. Move-centric analysis evaluates what each candidate
action creates and is structurally blind to what the given state already holds. On game
positions and analogous state-analysis tasks, first census every standing structure for
both sides, every partial line with its completion square and that square's enabling
condition, then evaluate candidates against the census. Stacked threats exist only at
census level.

## L53. Slots outrank ensembles, and hands outrank subjects (Q000053)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 23 and 24. On compound answers, per-slot evidence outranks
unstated ensemble priors: never demote a slot's leading read because another slot already
used that answer, and treat distinctness or curation assumptions as narrative-tier. On
attribution tasks, probe by technique fingerprint, whose hand made this, not by subject
association, who paints these things; the stem's own criterion word (style) is a flagged
hint subject-probing fails to consume. Honest joint pricing of compound recall validated
again.

## L54. Referent noise scales with componentry; defects are proven by offset reproduction (Q000054; Q000055)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 21 and 48. Multi-component hand-computed keys carry a
per-component author error surface, so compound exact-match items deserve a referent-noise
allowance below the single-value band even when every component is well-supported. When a
revealed key disagrees with a twice-derived value, difference the numbers first: a defect
mechanism often shows as an exact algebraic offset, and reproducing the key's number from
the correct value plus the mechanism settles oracle authority decisively. Redundant
differently-grouped arithmetic before commitment is what makes that demonstration possible.

## L55. Band from the decisive link's audited class; compute rivals to parity (Q000051; Q000056)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 21 and 29. The inference-defaults-low rule was under-applied
twice by starting from the strongest component and discounting. Banding must start from
the decisive link's claim type and that class's audited record, then argue upward with
named support. Separately, a live rival must be computed to the same standard as the
leader before ranking; a sloppily evaluated rival that is then demoted was never in the
candidate set, and one such miscomputation directly enabled a miss.

## L56. What worked: generators, redundancy, meaning-governed grading (Q000052; Q000057; Q000058; Q000060)

RIDM 16 surface: Sections 39 and 53. On items designed to be infeasible to execute, target
the generating pattern and verify each identification by a small direct reduction. On items
whose difficulty is sustained mechanical accuracy, spend the budget on a second
differently-structured verification pass. When a committed answer's surface matches a key
whose meaning the commitment contradicted, grade the meaning; crediting a string
coincidence would be false grounding applied to oneself. And when a question is coarser
than its scheme, determine explicitly whether the exotic elements can change the asked-for
answer at all.
